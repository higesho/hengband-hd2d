-----------------
The dual licence
-----------------
At the suggestion of Robert Ruehlmann, an effort has been under way to make
Angband and its variants distributable under the GPL. For details see the page
about the Angband OpenSource Initiative on the official Angband home page.

http://www.thangorodrim.net/development/opensource.html

In summary:

Angband and its predecessor Moria were written before the idea of OpenSource
was widely accepted, but their authors made a licence guaranteeing that
Angband and Moria would always be distributed free of charge.
(This is the so-called Moria/Angband licence.)

Copyright (c) 1997 Ben Harrison, James E. Wilson, Robert A. Koeneke

This software may be copied and distributed for educational, research,
and not for profit purposes provided that this copyright and statement
are included in all such copies.  Other copyrights may also apply.

That licence, however, does not meet the conditions of OpenSource: there is
no obligation to publish the source when distributing a changed version, and
there were several other problems besides. As a result Angband cannot lawfully
be included in a Linux distribution, nor can it use the many services prepared
for OpenSource projects (SourceForge and the like), nor can code distributed
under the GPL be used as part of Angband.

To overcome these problems, Robert Ruehlmann began the Angband OpenSource
Initiative, an effort to make it possible to distribute Angband -- and any
variant that wishes to share in the benefit of the licence change -- under the
GNU General Public License.

Changing the licence requires either the agreement of everyone who wrote any
of the code in Angband, or else a complete rewrite of every part written by an
author who has not agreed to the new licence.

Moving Angband to the GPL alone would force the authors of every variant based
on Angband to change their licence to the GPL as well. To avoid that, the
"dual licence" method was chosen, distributing the code under both the GPL and
the Angband licence. Variant authors may therefore go on using the Angband
licence if they wish; they need not change their variant's licence to the GPL.

Note that at present the dual licence applies only to the parts of the source
written by those who agreed to the Angband OpenSource Initiative. Other parts
are still distributed under the Moria/Angband licence alone, so the dual
licence does not affect the whole of Angband as it is distributed today. Until
every part of Angband can be distributed under both licences, the only licence
valid for the program as a whole is the Moria/Angband licence.

To distribute your own changes under the dual licence, you may use the short
statement below and send it by mail. (The statement must include your name; a
nickname is said to be acceptable.)

I hereby release [insert here the name of your patch, or the phrase 'all my
changes to the Angband code'], under the terms of the GNU General
Public License (version 2), as well as under the traditional Angband
license.  It may be redistributed under the terms of the GPL (version
2 or any later version), or under the terms of the traditional Angband
license.


You may also choose to release all your changes to Angband into the public
domain and give up all rights. If so, say so in your message.
