=== Exploring the dungeon ===

Keine: "Let me go over the dungeon in outline.
A dungeon is a set of parallel worlds whose shape changes utterly every time
you enter it. All manner of items lie scattered within, and vicious monsters
prowl about.

You move, attack, use items and do everything else by typing commands.
The enemies do not move until you act, and there is no time limit on your
input, so when things go badly do not panic -- take your time and think out
the best move.

Your journey's goal is to kill enemies and gain levels, to find items and
build up your equipment, and to kill the one behind the incident on the
bottom floor of the dungeon called Angband."


***** <Prepare>
=== Preparing to go down ===

Keine: "Now then. I understand your impatience, but go into the dungeon with
no preparation at all and you will soon be lost or killed.
Let me list what you should take into the earliest dungeons.

- Food
Reaching the place where a dungeon's entrance lies takes a journey of hours
or of days. Without enough food you will suffer the indignity of a game over
before you even arrive.
Food is sold at the general store in town.
A few rations are enough for a nearby dungeon, but a great eater such as an
oni had better carry many more.
A golem or a vampire cannot fill its stomach with ordinary food and must find
some other way.

- A light source
Some rooms in a dungeon are lit, but most are pitch dark.
In the dark you cannot see an enemy right beside you, and you can read
neither scrolls nor spellbooks.
To avoid that, wield a light source such as a torch or a lantern.
A torch gives so little light that you see only the grids next to you, so I
advise buying a lantern at the general store.
And do not forget flasks of oil to refill it.
A vampire sees in a pitch-dark room and needs no light source.

- Equipment
You have the barest equipment from the start, but that is a little thin.
Gather weapons and armour so the monsters of the dungeon do not kill you.

A class such as the Warrior starts with a decent weapon, but some classes
have nothing but a poor knife, or nothing at all.
Go to the weaponsmith and buy a weapon that suits your skill aptitudes.
Note that daggers and other short blades cannot be held in both hands and
gain blows only slowly, so I do not recommend them.

Next, buy a hat, boots, a cloak and so on at the armoury.
An AC of 20 or so is plenty down to about floor 10.
But putting on whatever looks hard is not the whole of it.
Carrying a shield tends to cost you blows, and heavy clothing makes spells
harder to cast. That choice depends on the case.

- A way home
Once you go deep, getting back is a labour in itself.
Then, deep in the dungeon, read a Scroll of Word of Recall and you return to
the surface. To go from the surface back into the dungeon, read the scroll
again or use a Divine Spirit Mausoleum branch office.
Recall takes some time to trigger, so it is no use as an emergency escape.

- Other things
Let me name some other tools that are useful early on. All are sold in the
town shops. You will not have the money to buy them all at once, so gather
them a little at a time while you make money on the shallow floors.
When something is sold out, wait a few hours of game time and the shop's
stock is renewed.

  - Scroll of Teleportation
    Read it and you move somewhere on the floor in an instant.
    Keep a few for when enemies surround you.

  - Potion of Cure Serious Wounds
    Carry ten or twenty and drink several at once when you have no room to
    rest. Drinking one also cures confusion and blindness.
    There is a stronger Potion of Cure Critical Wounds, but it costs rather
    more.

  - Scroll of Holy Chant
    Read it and you are blessed for a time.
    While blessed your defence and your chance to hit rise slightly, and your
    magic defence rises a little.
    Read one before fighting a strong enemy and things go rather more easily.

  - Scroll of Confuse Monster
    Read it and your hands begin to glow.
    Attack an enemy in that state and you can confuse it.
    This too is a genuinely useful thing when fighting a strong early enemy.

  - Staff or Scroll of Object Location
    Exploring a dungeon does not mean peering into every corner of every
    room; you would be there all day. Use one of these and you learn where
    the items lying nearby are.

  - Staff of Door/Stair Location, Scroll of Magic Mapping, Staff of
    Enlightenment
    These tell you where the nearby staircases are, or the shape of the
    ground. They cost a little, but you will want them by the time you go a
    good way down.

  - Ring of Warning
    Wear this on your finger and it trembles to warn you when you are about
    to step onto a trap, or when a strong enemy is likely to strike where you
    are going. A useful thing against early traps.

That is the gist of it. There are of course many other useful tools.
By the time you are past the beginning you will often carry Stone to Mud,
Teleport Away, a means of haste and a means of identification; from the
middle depths, double elemental resistance, powerful healing, an emergency
escape, full detection -- and a means of *Destruction*."


***** <MapSymbols>
=== Terrain symbols (in detail) ===

Keine: "I explained the letters and symbols when we spoke of the town, but in
the dungeon there are more of them and some change their meaning.
Let me go through them properly.
Type the '/' command and you can look up what a letter or symbol means.

Terrain first.

  [[[[w|.| (floor, road, sand, grass and so on)
  [[[[G|:| (flower beds, thickets and so on)
  Level ground. Ordinary terrain.
  Grass and sand of various colours have various names, but they work alike.
  [[[[w|^| (trap)
  A trap. Step onto it carelessly and it does you some harm.
  Traps are usually hidden and look just like the floor.
  You can find them by trap detection or by searching.
  A command lets you try to disarm one, though you may fail.
  No item ever lies on a trap.
  [[[[B|~| (shallow water)
  Water shallow enough to wade. A ningyo or a kappa is refreshed by it.
  Some monsters are aquatic and cannot leave the water.
  [[[[b|~| (deep water)
  Water deep enough that you must swim. A ningyo or a kappa is refreshed by
  it. Enter it without levitation, without resistance to water and with a
  heavy pack and you cannot swim -- you drown.
  While drowning, everything you do carries a penalty and you take damage.
  A vampire in particular drowns at once, without appeal, unless levitating.
  Many monsters cannot swim and will not enter deep water.
  No item ever lies on deep water.
  [[[[U|~| (shallow lava)
  Red-hot ground. Merely walking on it burns you.
  [[[[r|~| (deep lava)
  A lava flow. Enter it and you take heavy damage.
  Even levitating you take a little.
  Many monsters cannot enter lava either.
  No item ever lies on deep lava.
  [[[[D|#| (dark pit)
  A deep hole in the ground. You cannot enter it without levitation.
  No item ever lies in a pit.
  [[[[U|'| (open door)  [[[[u|'| (broken door)
  A door that stands open and can be passed.
  A door that is not broken can be closed with a command.
  No item ever lies where an unbroken door stands.
  [[[[U|+| (closed door)
  A door that is shut and cannot be passed.
  A door may be hidden and look just like a wall.
  It may be locked, or fixed so that it will never open.
  If it is locked you can try to pick it with a command.
  You can also smash it open by force, though I recommend that to none but the
  very strong.
  [[[[w|<| (up staircase)  [[[[w|>| (down staircase)
  [[[[U|<| (up shaft)      [[[[U|>| (down shaft)
  Staircases. Go up or down a floor with the '<' or '>' command.
  A staircase moves one level, a shaft two.
  No item ever lies on a staircase.
  Note that if you leave a floor and come back by the same staircase, the
  enemies' hit points have somehow been fully restored.
  [[[[G|#| (tree)
  Forest, rather than a single tree. You cannot see past it.
  Passing through it takes a little longer.
  (wall)
  It cannot be shown here, but it is a whitish square. It marks a dungeon
  wall. You cannot pass it, of course, though a ghost or a pass-wall spell
  can. With a high digging skill you can tunnel through it with a command; it
  takes a while. Quartz is easy to dig, granite rather harder.
  Any wall can be melted in an instant by Stone to Mud, whether cast or from a
  device. But an orange permanent wall can never be broken, and even a
  pass-wall spell cannot enter it.
  [[[[o|*| (vein with treasure)
  The same as a wall, but digging it out yields money.
  It is often disguised to look like an ordinary wall, but a Scroll of Detect
  Treasure and the like will find it.
  [[[[w|:| (rubble)
  A mass of stone. You cannot pass it, but it is fairly easy to dig.
  Digging it sometimes yields a lump of rock. If you are a golem, that is
  precious food.
  [[[[o|^| (mountain)
  An enormous wall. Mountains on the surface can be crossed by flying, but
  mountains inside a dungeon cannot be passed at all.
  1-9  (shops)
  There are eccentrics who keep shop inside the dungeon.
  You can trade there just as on the surface.

  There are other special terrains, but in the main a dungeon is built from
  the terrains listed above."

***** <Objects>
=== Item symbols ===

Keine: "Next, a short account of the letters items are shown by.
They are symbols, in the main.

$ (money)                    Money. It never weighs you down, however much
                             you carry.
& (chests, containers)       Open one and items come out. Often trapped.

! (drinks, bottles)          Liquor and potions. Sometimes other things
                             shaped like a bottle.
? (scrolls, spellbooks)      Scrolls and books.
, (food, mushrooms)          Eat it to fill your stomach. Some have special
                             effects.
_ (staves)                   Using one has various effects. It has a set
                             number of charges.
- (wands, rods)              Using one has various effects. Several can be
                             carried together.

| (swords, knives, blades)   Edged weapons.
\ (hafted weapons, axes)     Heavy weapons.
/ (poles, spears, polearms)  Long weapons.
{ (bows, crossbows, guns)    Missile launchers.
} (arrows, shot and so on)   Ammunition.

( (clothing, cloaks)         Light armour worn on the body or over it.
) (hats, gloves, boots)      Light armour worn on head, hands and feet.
[ (body armour, shields)     Heavy armour worn on the body or the arm.
] (helms, gauntlets, greaves) Heavy armour worn on head, hands and feet.
" (amulets)                  Ornaments worn round the neck.
= (rings)                    Ornaments worn on a finger.
~ (light sources, ribbons)   Torches and lanterns, or ribbon-shaped ornaments.

* (materials and so on)      Materials, ores, and other round things.
` (figurines)                Magical figurines. Throw one and it takes shape
                             as your servant.
~ (photographs)              A photograph with somebody in it.

That is about all. As I said, some monsters look like items.
Beware in particular of the monster called the Death Sword, which looks like
a '|'."


***** <Feelings>
=== The feeling of a level ===

Keine: "Explore a dungeon for a while and you may sense something of the
feeling of the floor you are on:
"What a boring place..."
"This place seems reasonably safe."
"This place does not seem too risky."
"This place seems terribly dangerous." and so on.
What is it that produces this feeling?

It is an item, or a monster, out of keeping with the floor you are on.
Explore for a while on a floor that feels dangerous and you may find an
unbelievably precious item lying on the ground -- or you may run headlong
into an enemy you cannot handle.
On such a floor you should explore more carefully than usual.
If you have a means of detecting items or monsters, use it often.

The higher your searching skill, the sooner you sense the feeling of a floor.
And if you are a lucky man (or girl), or have set certain options, the
messages you see will differ from the ones I just gave."


***** <Vault>
=== Special rooms and vaults ===

Keine: "You may find strange structures in the dungeon.
Let me name a few.

- Pit / Nest (a monster den)
  A corridor runs all the way round a rectangular room, and the room inside
  is packed tight with monsters.
  Every monster inside is awake from the start.
  I do not know what they are doing in there, but open the door carelessly
  and they may all attack you at once.
  The shape is distinctive, so if you are watchful you should know one from
  outside.

- Vault (a treasure chamber)
  What people call a monster house.
  The room is crammed with items, traps and monsters.
  The shape is complicated, and some are enormous, like a castle.
  Most are enclosed by permanent walls that cannot be broken, and the
  monsters inside are plainly stronger than the floor you are on.
  Until you are used to exploring, the wise course is to keep away.

- Horde
  Not quite a structure, but the dungeon sometimes holds a crowd of awake
  monsters that all share a symbol.
  Monsters stronger than the floor are often mixed in, which makes it quite
  dangerous. Deep down in particular, a careless teleport can drop you into
  the middle of a pack of dragons or hounds.

- Arcade (a shopping street)
  For some reason there is sometimes a town inside the dungeon.
  Make use of it without thinking about it too hard. Bargains seem easier to
  find in the deep floors.
  Merely entering a shop counts as an action, so clear the nearby enemies
  first.

- Pattern (a floor with a pattern drawn on it)
  The details are unknown, but it seems to be a device that sets some kind of
  trial. You will see one inside certain enormous vaults.
  It is a single unbroken path from a starting point to an exit.
  You cannot step across into it and you cannot leave partway.
  What is more, your HP keeps falling while you walk it.
  Reach the exit and you can, it seems, teleport to whatever floor you like.
  Fine items are laid there too."


***** <RandomQuests>
=== Random quests ===

Keine: "On certain floors of Angband a random quest occurs.
A monster of some name stands guard there; kill it and a staircase down
appears, together with several fine items as your reward.
If you cannot kill it you may flee the floor, though of course the quest then
fails."

--- Yukari Yakumo ---
Keine: "On floor 50 of Angband the great youkai Yukari Yakumo has taken up
her place. First, fight her and kill her. She is a formidable enemy, but
there is always an opening.
On easy mode, killing Yukari Yakumo wins the game.
On normal mode, killing her makes you a winner, and you may end the game
there if you wish."


***** <Dungeons>
=== The kinds of dungeon ===
Keine: "Gensokyo holds a variety of dungeons. Let me present them.

- Angband (floors 1 to 100)
  The main stage of your adventure.
  Conquering it is your goal.
  Its monsters and terrain are the standard ones.
  Gems and ores are sometimes found, according to the depth.

- Cave of the Great Mausoleum (floors 1 to 30)
  A dungeon reached from Myouren Temple, near the Human Village.
  No special terrain such as vaults appears, and no enemy stronger than the
  floor. It makes a fine training ground for the start of an adventure.
  Deeper in, gems are sometimes found.

- Forest of Magic Depths (floors 24 to 36)
  A dungeon deep in the Forest of Magic.
  Almost all the terrain is forest, grass, marsh and rubble.
  Beasts and giant mushrooms appear readily.
  Mushrooms seem to be gathered here in quantity.
  An enormous mushroom is said to hold the deepest part.

- Genbu Ravine (floors 10 to 20)
  Between the Forest of Magic and Youkai Mountain.
  It is built of pitted walls and rivers.
  Aquatic creatures appear often. Rare materials seem to be gathered here.

- Scarlet Devil Mansion Depths (floors 30 to 50)
  Entered from the Scarlet Devil Mansion, which stands by the lake
  north-northwest of the Human Village.
  It is built of comparatively orderly walls and corridors.
  The enemies seem mostly humanoid and demonic.
  Magical devices are often found.
  The deepest part holds the mistress of the mansion, Remilia Scarlet.
  And near the bottom the head maid will cut you down without a word.
  Both are highly dangerous, so unless you are sure of yourself, do not go far
  in.

- Hell Valley (floors 35 to 50)
  A good way northwest of the Human Village, near the foot of Youkai Mountain.
  It is full of rock and pits, so exploring is hard without levitation.
  Ghostly enemies appear often.
  Materials essential to magic seem to be found here.
  A hell crow of the underground guards the deepest part.

- Muenzuka (floors 40 to 50)
  A good way northeast of the Human Village, beyond Youkai Mountain.
  Open terrain with much wood and grass.
  All kinds of materials and fine items are often found, but enemies far too
  strong for the depth are often awake from the start.

- Former Blazing Hell (floors 40 to 50)
  Entered from the courtyard of the Palace of the Earth Spirits, in the
  Former Hell Road. To reach the Former Hell Road you must walk southwest
  from the Human Village and then a good way east.
  But there is a rumour of a road in the Forest of Magic that leads to the
  Former Hell Road. It may be worth looking for.

  The Former Blazing Hell is lava from end to end. Entering it without
  levitation and without protection from fire is suicide.
  Many enemies resist fire or fly.

- Inverted Castle (floors 30 to 45)
  A good way northwest of the Human Village.
  A castle floating in the air, the stage of the incident once raised by a
  descendant of Issun-boushi and an amanojaku.
  Corridors are often broken by deep pits, so levitation is essential.
  For some reason no magic at all can be used here.

- Senkai (floors 30 to 45)
  A good way west of the Human Village.
  A place the saint is said to have made as a training ground for hermits.
  A curious place where every wall is glass.
  For some reason no melee attack at all can be used here.

- Dragon's Lair (floors 60 to 72)
  Underground, further in from the Former Hell Road.
  A dangerous place where vicious dragons from another world appear in
  numbers. It is full of pits, so walking is hard without levitation.
  Rare gems can be had, they say, but it is not a place to go deep into for
  pleasure.

- Dream World (floors 30 to 70)
  The entrance lies deep in Youkai Mountain, near the lake.
  This is properly the world of dreams that everyone visits while asleep, but
  it seems one can enter it in the flesh.
  Since you are already inside a dream, everyone is awake, and on top of that
  it is thick with vaults and other special structures -- an extremely
  dangerous place. Materials found nowhere else can be had, but unless you
  are very sure of yourself you had better not go.

- Hell (floors 40 to 80)
  Pass Muenzuka, cross the Sanzu River where plesiosaurs and giant fish come
  at you, and you reach Higan. The entrance to Hell is there.
  Inside it teems with demons and undead and is full of damaging terrain.
  It is not a place to go without pressing business.

- Rainbow Dragon Cave (floors 30 to 65)
  Go north from the Human Village and climb the mountain and you come to an
  open place called False Tentana. The entrance to the Rainbow Dragon Cave is
  there. Inside it is a narrow but deep dungeon, and rare ores are said to be
  found in its depths.
  There is something to beware of: this place is said to be short of the
  substance called oxygen, so humans cannot breathe.
  It gives me no trouble at all, mind. Curious, is it not.
  A human, or one of the races close to humans, will come to grief without
  some item to make up the oxygen.
  A ring that keeps you from drowning, perhaps?"


***** <Souvenir>
=== What each dungeon yields ===

Keine: "As I have just explained, the dungeons of each region sometimes yield
produce of their own.
Mushrooms are often found in the Forest of Magic, mercury and arsenic in Hell
Valley, magical devices in the Scarlet Devil Mansion.
Muenzuka seems to yield a thoroughly mixed bag.
The higher your searching ability, the more readily these things are found."
