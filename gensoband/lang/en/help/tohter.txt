***** <Mutation>
=== Mutations ===

Keine: "Now and then, while exploring, you may suffer a mutation.
A mutation is a change worked upon part of your body or mind.
Some are charming enough -- cat ears, say -- and some are useful and raise a
stat, but a few are dangerous enough to end the game if you do not treat them
quickly.

Mutations come about mainly like this:
- taking a chaos attack without resistance to chaos gives a random mutation
- drinking a Potion of Mutation very likely gives several mutations
- spells such as Self-Transformation and Body Transformation give mutations
- an attack that brings on insanity has a small chance of giving a harmful one

You recover from mutations mainly like this:
- treatment at Eientei removes one random mutation ($3000)
- drinking a Potion of Mutation has a small chance of removing them all
- "Rebirth" or "Race Transformation", by certain potions and spells, removes
  them all

Bear this in mind:
having mutations slows the natural regeneration of your HP and MP a little,
in proportion to how many you have.
There is a technique of taking chaos attacks on purpose to gather useful
mutations, but do not carry it too far or your exploring will suffer.

Note that a few races -- the oni's horns, the mermaid's tail -- have from the
start what amounts to a mutation of their own.
These bodily traits of the race never go away, and they do not affect your
rate of recovery."


--- Race transformation ---
Keine: "Magic or a curse of the greatest power can change your very race.
You will rarely see it. The main causes are these:

- while under the patronage of the gods of Chaos, their whim may transform you
- spells such as Self-Transformation have a small chance of transforming you
- casting Reincarnation in the Transform realm, or Ghostly Ogre-God in the
  Necromancy realm
- wearing certain special artifacts (you are warned first)

After a transformation your current level is recalculated against the new
race's experience factor.
As for your score, it is worked out with the lowest score multiplier among
all the races you have ever been. Winning *the game* as a strong race and then
turning into a weak one before you finish will not raise your score.

A few races such as the youkai doll and the stray god are never transformed.
And if you belong to a unique class you should never be transformed except by
the Reincarnation spell and by artifacts."


***** <Extra_mode>
=== EXTRA mode ===

Keine: "Version 1.0.99 added the difficulty "EXTRA".
In EXTRA mode your adventure has an entirely different flavour.
Let me set out the differences in detail.

- You start on floor 1 of Angband and can only go down
	The game begins not in the Human Village but at a random spot on floor 1
        of Angband. There are only down staircases, and Teleport Level also take
        s
	you only downward.
	You cannot leave the dungeon with recall magic or a recall item, and
	floor-regenerating magic such as Alter Reality or Shadow Shift has no
	effect. The shafts that carry you down two floors at once do not appear.
	Win in exactly one hundred floors, with no second chances.

- You level up quickly
	In EXTRA mode the experience needed for the next level is halved.
	Unless your character carries a very heavy experience penalty you will
	never want for levels. The youkai doll needs the same amount of
	reinforcement as in other difficulties, but good equipment turns up
	readily, so it too will advance quickly.

- Good items are common, and some consumables come in stacks
        In EXTRA mode items of a higher level than the floor you are on are ofte
        n
	lying about. Fine equipment and artifacts come easily, and potions,
	scrolls and wands are often found several at a time.
	The chance that a particular unique monster drops a particular fixed
	artifact is doubled.

- "Item cards" exist
	EXTRA mode has strange cards with pictures on them of such things as the
	yin-yang orb or the hakkero.
        Nothing happens while they sit in your pack; use the U command and you c
        an
	set up to eight of them in dedicated slots and invoke them from there.
	Invoking one looses an ability such as Fantasy Seal or Master Spark.
	It costs no MP and never fails, but each card is used up once invoked.
	Many are useful not only for attack but for defence and support.
	Read a card with the r command and you can learn what it does.

	These cards are dropped with moderate probability by unique monsters.
	Kill one of the famous of Gensokyo and you will very likely get a card
	connected with her.
        Cards are also found lying on the floor. As with the produce of a dungeo
        n,
	a high searching ability seems to find them more easily.

	In EXTRA mode you will be short of one thing or another at every turn.
	Use the item cards well and see yourself through.

	(The cards are called ability cards in the shops and item cards in your
	pack; they are the same thing.)

- "Extra quests" occur
	No random quests occur in the Angband of EXTRA mode.
	Instead, nine special quests occur on floors 10, 20, 30 ... 90.
        Descend to one of those floors and it is laid out like a particular ques
        t
        dungeon; kill the enemies on the floor and the quest is complete, and th
        e
	reward appears near the staircase.

        The staircase on such a floor leads down, and going down before the ques
        t
        is complete counts as abandoning it. Teleport Level likewise abandons th
        e
	quest and drops you to the floor below.
        As in an ordinary quest dungeon, *Destruction* and Genocide cannot be us
        ed.
	Enemies that summon are best killed quickly, one way or another.

- Special buildings appear on the floor
	On floors with no quest there is a small building somewhere.
	It may be a shop such as a weaponsmith, or it may hold a special service
	such as remove curse, identify or recharging.
        How often these buildings appear varies a little with the floor you are
        on
        and with your class. Some of them are vital, so look for them and call i
        n
	when you can.
        Enemies occasionally appear inside and set upon you without warning, so
        I
        do not recommend rummaging through suspicious places when your HP is lo
        w.

- The floors are laid out a little differently
        Sometimes a floor resembles the Forest of Magic or Muenzuka, or takes so
        me
	other distinctive shape.
	Floors that are far too cramped do not appear.
	The rectangular monster rooms -- pits and nests -- are less common, and
	underground towns a little more common.

- Your score is four times NORMAL and twice HARD
        Since there are fewer quests, the bonus for completing quests and the bo
        nus
	for depth reached are higher than in the other modes.
	Classes that suit the EXTRA system very badly -- and, conversely, a few
	classes that lose little to its restrictions -- receive a special
	adjustment to the score multiplier.
        Some birth options carry a different score multiplier here than elsewher
        e.

- Other
	The shops on a floor carry little stock, and it is never renewed however
	long you wait.

	Some unique monsters that appear only in particular quests in the other
	difficulties turn up in the dungeon here as a matter of course.
	Beware the chief of the youkai tanuki in particular.

        Non-unique monsters that appear on a floor anew, through summoning or wi
        th
	the passing of time, yield few items and little experience when killed.
	In other words, having a summoner call up weaklings, or lingering on a
	floor you have already cleared, is a poorer way of making a living.


That is about all.
To win in EXTRA mode you will need not only a good character and good strategy
but a certain amount of luck.
I wish you well."

--- How to use a base of operations in EXTRA mode ---

Keine: "In EXTRA mode you cannot return to a town to use your base of
operations. That is the hard part of EXTRA mode.
There are, however, several ways to use the base within the dungeon.
It is no exaggeration to say that securing them decides whether the adventure
succeeds. Let me set them out here.

- Use an item card
	The item cards "Twisted Parasol" and "Magic Vial" let you use your base.

        The parasol is the stronger: it opens a door to the base where you stan
        d,
	but it cannot be used on a floor where a quest is under way.
	Nor can it be used on the deepest floor before the boss is killed, so
	settle your preparations before you descend to it.
	Once a quest is complete you may use it even inside the fixed dungeon of
	an extra quest.

        The vial can be used at any time, but its effect ends the moment you lea
        ve
        the base. Unlike the parasol, it will not let you strew items on the flo
        or
	and sort through them at leisure.
        You also cannot change the item cards in your slots while the base is op
        en.
	Think carefully about what goes in and what comes out before you use it.

        Item cards are had by killing unique monsters or by raising your searchi
        ng
	ability and combing the floor.
        These two are somewhat rare and hard to come by, but Yukari Yakumo and,
        in
        the middle depths and below, Marisa Kirisame have a relatively good chan
        ce
	of dropping them.
        If one lies on the floor early on, the feeling of the floor changes enou
        gh
	that you will not miss it.

- Send items to the base with magic or an item
	Certain artifacts and spells send items to your base.
	The Summon, Nature and Chaos realms hold such spells.
        This is a one-way transfer to the base, however; you cannot take anythin
        g
        out. Some of the means have a weight limit, and some have a small chance
        of
	destroying the item.

- Use a facility on the floor
        EXTRA mode puts a small building on floors with no quest, and some of th
        em
	will exchange items with your base.

        The "Divine Spirit Mausoleum branch office" will send items to your hom
        e.

	At "An Open Place", where the youkai Rin Kaenbyou is found, items can be
	carried to your home, and one item can be fetched from it.

	At "A Strange Place", where the barrier is frayed, you may with luck be
	allowed to use your home once. Fail, however, and you are swallowed by a
	gap and suffer a mutation. Nimble fingers seem to help.

	And the room of a zashiki-warashi working remotely likewise lets you use
	your home once. This one works for certain as long as you find the
	building, which is reassuring -- but it is rather hard to find."


***** <Gun>
=== Weapons of the "gun" class ===

Keine: "From version 1.1.04 the weapons of the "gun" class work very
differently. In a word, they are like a rod you wear.
Let me explain the new rules for guns.
The commands are given in the original keyset.

- The basics
	Wield one in your right or left hand and shoot with the 'f' command, not
	with 'A'. After the command you choose a target, and once the target is
	set the roll to succeed is made.
	On a success the shot is fired at the target.
        Energy is spent either way, and once you have used up your rounds the gu
        n
	cannot be used until at least one round has been loaded again.

- Rounds
	Every gun has a number of rounds.
	 *Evolving Gun 'Crimson' (0d0) (+20,+20) (+4 speed)(6/6)
	 The (6/6) at the end is the rounds. Each shot takes it to (5/6), (4/6)
	 and so on.

- Two guns at once
	You may wield a different gun in each hand.
	Then, after you choose a target, each gun makes its own roll and its own
        shot. The energy cost is as usual, but the chance to succeed drops a goo
        d
	deal. Gauntlets or gloves with a two-weapon bonus soften the penalty.
	Note that if the guns you wield are too heavy, neither hand can shoot.

- The chance to hit
	The chance depends on the strength of the gun being fired, on the higher
	of your shooting skill and your magic device skill, on your gun
	proficiency, on your dexterity, on the to-hit bonuses of your armour and
	accessories, and on the gun's own to-hit bonus.
	Holding a single gun in both hands gives a bonus to the chance.
	Fear, confusion and firing two guns lower it sharply.
        If a powerful gun falls into the hands of a class with little aptitude f
        or
	guns or shooting, I am afraid you cannot rely on it very far.

- Shortening the reload
	After a shot a gun is reloading and cannot be used, and the reload time
	varies with the kind of gun.
        Certain conditions shorten it: gun proficiency at the fourth rank, a rap
        id
	fire property on the gun, a Ring of Extra Shots on the hand holding the
	gun, and the trigger-happy nature.
	With all of them together the reload completes some four or five times
	faster than usual.

- The damage of a gun
	A gun's damage comes purely from the gun itself.
	Except for certain fixed artifacts it is simply the dice plus the damage
	bonus.
        Some guns fire with an element such as fire or holy attached, and then t
        he
	damage rises or falls with the enemy's resistances and weaknesses.

- Checking the details of your shooting
        The hit chance and the reload time of the gun you are wielding can be se
        en
	on the status screen with the 'C' command.
        And if you *identify* a gun and look at it with the I command you can se
        e
	the element of its shot and so on.

- Fighting hand to hand while wielding a gun
	When you wield a gun and carry no other melee weapon, you can attack
	adjacent enemies with the gun.
	This counts as a martial arts attack, so your martial arts proficiency,
	your strength and your dexterity all come into it.
        Sometimes this fires a point-blank shot from a gun that is still reloadi
        ng;
	please do not think about that too hard.
        The "rifle" has a bayonet fixed to it, so this attack is stronger with o
        ne.

- Reflection
        If, when you look at a gun with the I command, its effect is written as
        a
	"bolt", some enemies can reflect it.
	If it says only "shot", it is never reflected.

- Ego items and random artifacts
	Guns now appear as ego items and as random artifacts.
	The element or the kind of the shot can change when they do.
        A gun whose dice have risen sharply usually shoots with an element, so m
        ind
	the enemy's resistances when you use it.

- The "no guns appear" option
	Turn on the "no guns appear" option during play and weapons of the gun
	class stop appearing.
	You can turn it on and off at any time, and there is neither penalty nor
        bonus. If you feel the smell of gunpowder does not suit Gensokyo, turn i
        t
	on."



***** <Score_server>
=== The score server ===

Keine: "When you retire or when the game is over you may send your game data
to the score server. Do tell everyone the tale of your adventure.

Note that Gensoband's score server, unlike Hengband's, receives the save file
itself. The plan is to use that data for various rankings and statistics in
future.

I doubt anyone does, but if you keep a password or other confidential
information in the game's note function, delete it before you send.
If you do not wish to send your score, press N when asked "Send your score?",
or turn score sending off from the play options in the option settings.



***** <Mana_shield>
=== The "barrier guard" ===

Keine: "From version 1.1.27 on, a spellcasting class that uses the Ctrl-'g'
command enters the "barrier guard" state.
While it holds, half of the damage from an enemy's attack goes to your MP
rather than your HP. Even a caster, whose HP tends to be low, will find the
middle depths easier to walk.

But take too much damage at once, or let your MP reach zero, and the guard
"breaks" and leaves you stunned.
The barrier also disappears if you are paralysed, confused, dazed, heavily
stunned, or under an anti-magic effect.

Note that from version 1.1.96 on, when you use the barrier guard, a "reduce
spell difficulty" property makes it one and a half times as resistant to
breaking, and a "reduce mana cost" property cuts the MP spent by 25%.
