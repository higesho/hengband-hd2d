I:0
J:ソルジャー
D:The Warrior is the basic class for male units. It has no special skills of
D:its own, and its parameters rise evenly, so a unit raised as a Warrior to
D:the end tends to become a jack-of-all-trades. It is quite useful, however,
D:for correcting a character who has been over-specialized.

I:1
J:ナイト
D:The Knight is an apostle of chivalry, excelling in martial skill and
D:prizing loyalty. Knights cannot use magic, but have better defense than
D:Warriors and are skilled with swords and pole weapons. They are fair
D:riders, and the attack of a mounted Knight wielding a lance is devastating.

I:2
J:バーサーカー
D:The Berserker is a ferocious warrior who cuts a path through the carnage
D:with a robust body and superhuman power. Berserkers favor axes and blunt
D:weapons. They cannot use magic and are poor with magic devices, but their
D:battle-honed instincts are keen, and they excel at disarming, perception
D:and searching.

I:3
J:テラーナイト
D:The Terror Knight is a fearsome knight who proudly leads the spirits of
D:everyone he has slain. His killing power is nothing short of mythic. His
D:magical ability, on the other hand, can be called nonexistent.

I:4
J:ビーストテイマー
D:The Beast Tamer is a warrior who fights alongside magical beasts. Beast
D:Tamers favor the whip and can cast Symbiotic magic that supports their
D:pets. They are also skilled riders, and suffer little penalty for equipping
D:weapons ill suited to riding. The ability needed to cast spells is Wisdom.

I:5
J:ソードマスター
D:The Swordmaster is a seeker who polishes his soul by mastering the sword,
D:striving to overcome his karma. He has superb swordsmanship, excels at
D:wielding two swords, and can cast magic of his own element. The ability
D:needed to cast spells is Intelligence.

I:6
J:ドラグーン
D:A class that favors swords and boasts overwhelming killing power against
D:dragons. Dragoons can also use simple magic. The ability needed to cast
D:spells is Intelligence.

I:7
J:ニンジャ
D:The Ninja is an excellent warrior of darkness with high stealth and great
D:swordsmanship, who can fight with two weapons and also use magic of his own
D:element. The ability needed to cast spells is Intelligence.

I:8
J:ウィザード
D:The Wizard is a mage who makes full use of many attack spells, drawing on
D:broad knowledge and rich experience. In addition to Magery, Wizards can
D:wield the magic of the four elements. The ability needed to cast spells is
D:Intelligence.

I:9
J:ウォーロック
D:The Warlock is a scholarly mage. Warlocks master Witch magic, two books of
D:Magery, and Drakonite magic. They have a fair amount of melee power, and
D:can summon golems. The ability needed to cast spells is Intelligence.

I:10
J:エクソシスト
D:The Exorcist is a specialist in dispelling demons and evil spirits. By
D:wielding Crusade magic, Exorcists show unmatched strength against evil
D:demons and the undead. They also have fair melee power, and can find a way
D:out through blows. The ability needed to cast spells is Wisdom.

I:11
J:アマゾネス
D:The Amazoness is the basic class for female units. She cannot use magic,
D:but handles everything else with ease. She is clearly lacking in strength,
D:however, so raising her as an Amazoness to the very end is not advisable.

I:12
J:ヴァルキリー
D:The Valkyrie is a spear-wielding magic warrior who is also a good rider. A
D:spear thrust from horseback can be devastating. The ability needed to cast
D:spells is Wisdom.

I:13
J:アーチャー
D:The Archer is a specialist with bows. Archers cannot use magic, but their
D:shooting ability is immense. They can shoot down enemy after enemy.

I:14
J:ドラゴンテイマー
D:The Dragon Tamer is a magic warrior who takes up the sword herself and
D:fights alongside dragons. Dragons are especially fond of her, and she can
D:borrow their power at any time. The ability needed to cast spells is
D:Wisdom.

I:15
J:ウィッチ
D:The Witch is a sorceress skilled in support magic and the use of magic
D:devices. She can cast Witch magic, Magery, and the first book of Death
D:magic. The ability needed to cast spells is Intelligence.

I:16
J:セイレーン
D:The Siren is a beautiful witch who wields many attack spells. In practice
D:she is almost no different from a Wizard. The ability needed to cast spells
D:is Intelligence.

I:17
J:クレリック
D:The Cleric is a member of the clergy skilled in healing magic. Clerics can
D:cast Holy magic and magic of their own element. Compared with other
D:spellcasters, they can also use bows to a degree. A Cleric may be regarded
D:as an apprentice class until changing to Priest. The ability needed to cast
D:spells is Wisdom.

I:18
J:プリースト
D:The Priest is a member of the clergy who makes full use of both attack and
D:healing magic. Priests can cast Holy and Crusade magic and magic of their
D:own element. Think of it as the advanced class of Cleric. Their healing
D:magic is somewhat inferior to that of a Cleric. The ability needed to cast
D:spells is Wisdom.

I:19
J:リッチ
D:A reincarnation class that Wizards, Warlocks, Exorcists, Sirens and Witches
D:equipped with the Ring of the Dead are reborn into after death. Having
D:returned through the power of the Ring, they have acquired even
D:higher-level abilities. They can use every realm of magic except Holy,
D:Symbiotic, Crusade and Drakonite, but can no longer change class. The
D:ability needed to cast spells is Intelligence. Liches do not need food.

I:20
J:エンジェルナイト
D:A reincarnation class that a pure-hearted female unit may be reborn into
D:after death. Divine Knights boast high combat ability in both melee and
D:magic, but can no longer change class. They can use Crusade magic and magic
D:of their own element. The ability needed to cast spells is Wisdom. Divine
D:Knights do not need food.

J:巫女
D:Mediums are maidens devoted to the gods. Frail, they are poor at fighting
D:enemies directly, but arrows they loose with a focused mind never miss. At
D:higher levels they can imbue arrows with a holy power that fells trees and
D:pierces foes. They can also purify evil defilement by cleansing themselves
D:on water, and can chant prayers to the gods. Mediums shun anything but
D:weapons blessed by the gods, and shields that hinder drawing the bow;
D:equipping these disturbs their prayer and makes their arrows fly poorly.
D:The ability needed for magic is Wisdom.

J:サキュバス
D:The Succubus is a witch who has become a creature of the demon realm
D:through a pact with demons. She can cast Witch and Death magic. This class

I:34
J:グラップラー

D:The Knight Commander is an elite knight who leads the Dark Knights
D:Loslorien. You can be promoted from a Templar when you and your two
D:companions are all alive and the Lodissian Chaos Frame is 150 or higher.
