# TOband -> English: race (p_race) descriptions. UTF-8; everything except the J: lines is ASCII.
# I: p_race number / J: the Japanese race name on the N: line (must match) / D: English description.
# Race names come from the glossary (classes_races.tsv).

I:0
J:人間
D:The Human is the baseline character; all other races are compared to
D:Humans. Humans can take any class and perform averagely in every one.
D:Because of their short life span, they tend to gain levels faster than any
D:other race. They have no special modifiers or traits.

I:1
J:ホークマン
D:Hawkmen are a fierce, battle-loving winged race. They are long-lived, with
D:three times the life span of humans. By using their wings they can avoid
D:pit traps and dangerous terrain.

I:2
J:リザードマン
D:Lizardmen are an amphibious race with hard scales. Their scales give them
D:resistance to acid and cold. They are also excellent fighters with a high
D:rate of HP growth, and are reasonably competent at disarming traps and
D:using magic devices.

I:3
J:フェアリー
D:Faeries are very small. They have tiny wings and can fly over traps and
D:dangerous terrain. They love sunlight and resist light, but take great
D:damage from darkness. At high levels they can fly faster.

I:4
J:グレムリン
D:Gremlins are the opposite of Faeries. They have wings and can fly over
D:traps and dangerous terrain. They love darkness and resist dark and nether,
D:but take great damage from light. At high levels they can fly faster.

I:5
J:スケルトン
D:As undead, Skeletons need not worry about poison or life-draining attacks.
D:They do not use eyes to perceive objects, so they are not fooled by things
D:unseen. Their bones resist shards and the like, and at higher levels they
D:gain resistance to cold. The effects of potions and food take hold without
D:passing through a Skeleton's stomach (which does not exist), but the
D:potions and food themselves fall through the jaw, so no nourishment can be
D:absorbed. Skeletons do not need food.

I:6
J:ゴースト
D:Ghosts are a powerful kind of undead. Being semi-material, they can pass
D:through walls, but are hurt by the wall's density when they do. Like other
D:undead, they cannot be drained of life, can see invisible things, resist
D:poison and cold, and are immune to nether. At sufficiently high levels they
D:gain telepathy. Ghosts can become superb spellcasters, but their physical
D:traits are very poor. Ghosts do not need food.

I:7
J:パンプキンヘッド
D:Pumpkinheads are pumpkin ghouls created by Deneb the Witch. They provoke
D:the cruelty of their enemies, so enemies target them first. Pumpkinheads
D:are physically weak, but are immune to paralysis, resist chaos and
D:disenchantment, sustain their life force, and gain telepathy as they level
D:up.

I:8
J:ゴブリン
D:Goblins make good warriors, but do not expect magic from them. They are
D:poor at stealth, and terrible at searching, disarming and perception.
D:Goblins are ugly and tend to be charged higher prices in shops. Since they
D:like living underground, they resist darkness.

I:9
J:ゴーゴン
D:Gorgons are monsters with countless snakes growing in place of hair and,
D:instead of a mermaid's tail, the lower body of a snake. They are competent
D:in all skills, and are far better with bows than other races. They resist
D:petrification, and as they level up they also gain resistance to acid,
D:cold, poison, nether and chaos. In addition, they can use the evil eye,
D:which turns those who see it to stone, making them an extremely powerful
D:race.

I:10
J:マーメイド
D:Mermaids are a sea-dwelling race, human above the waist and fish below.
D:They favor water and can be expected to fight well in it. On land, however,
D:they are weak and move with difficulty. They are also excellent singers and
D:can sing songs that charm monsters.

I:11
J:マッドハロウィン
D:Mad Halloweens are a race of Pumpkinheads strengthened by rotten pumpkins.
D:They are physically superior to Pumpkinheads.

I:12
J:バルタン
D:Descendants of the ancient winged people said to have ruled the heavens.
D:Their burning red hair is the mark of their noble blood.

I:13
J:レイブン
D:Winged people who fell to the dark path, soaring on jet-black feathers. An
D:extremely ferocious race that loves blood more than strife itself.
