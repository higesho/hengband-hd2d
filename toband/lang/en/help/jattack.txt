=== Attacking and Weapons ===

Attacking is simple in TOband. If you move into a monster, you attack
it. If you are wielding a weapon (including digging implements, which
are considered to be weapons) when you do so, the damage of the weapon
is added when you hit a monster. If you wield nothing, you will attack
using martial arts.

Melee can do more damage per turn than any other form of attack, and
the basic equipment (a weapon) is easy to find. On the other hand,
melee only works against adjacent monsters and takes a great deal of
good equipment to come into its own deeper in the dungeon. Upgrading
to weapons with higher base damage dice is vital, but heavy weapons
are harder to master. You will have to find a compromise, depending on
class, experience level, and available equipment (use the 'C'haracter
screen to see how various weapons affect your melee skill).

You may wield both a primary weapon for melee combat and a bow or
other missile launcher for attacking from a distance at the same time.
Most classes will also benefit from carrying an assortment of magical
devices for attack.

***** <DistanceAttack>
--- Attacking from a Distance ---

You can attack monsters from a distance by firing a missile from a bow
or other missile launcher, by throwing an object or by using magical
items such as wands, staffs and rods. If you have chosen to play a
spell casting class, you may be able to learn some spells which allow
you to attack a monster from a distance. You can use distance attacks
even when your target is next to you.

Whenever you give a command to fire a weapon, cast a spell, or use an
attacking magical device (unless the spell or device has an area
effect), you will be prompted for a direction. You may choose any of
the eight movement directions or press '*' or the space key to enter
targeting mode. A detailed explanation of targeting mode can be found
in the section on Command Descriptions
(see jcommdesc.txt#ThrowFire [b]).

You may also wish to make use of the use_old_target option, which
automatically selects the last target. This saves you from having to
target the same monster every time you attack it. An explanation of
this option is found in the section on User Interface Options
(see joption.txt#use_old_target [c]).

***** <AttackWalls>
--- Attacking Monsters in Walls ---

Some monsters, for example ghosts, can pass through the dungeon walls.
Even when such a monster is inside a wall, you can attack it by trying
to move into the wall space which contains the monster. If the monster
is invisible and you do not have the ability to see invisible
creatures, you must tunnel into the wall space containing the monster
to attack it.

Bolt spells such as Magic Missile do full damage to a monster in a
wall, but ball spells such as Mana Burst will be stopped and blow up
just in front of the wall, and do only half damage. Inversely, when
you have the ability to pass through walls and are inside one, this
becomes a very important privilege for you.


***** <MeleeWeapons>
=== Melee Weapons ===

Carrying a weapon in your backpack does you no good. You must wield a
weapon before it can be used in a fight. A secondary weapon can be kept
in the backpack, and switched with the primary weapon when needed.

Weapons have two main magical characteristics, their enchanted ability
to hit and their enchanted ability to do damage, expressed as
"(+#,+#)". A normal weapon would be "(+0,+0)". Many weapons have
bonuses to hit and/or to do damage. Some weapons are cursed, and have
penalties that hurt the player. Cursed weapons cannot be unwielded
until the curse is lifted. Identifying a weapon will tell you the
magical bonuses and penalties and whether or not it is cursed.

TOband assumes that your youth in the rough environment near the
dungeons has taught you the relative merits of different weapons. The
damage dice which define a weapon's capabilities are displayed as part
of its description. Any damage enchantment is added to the dice roll
for that weapon. The dice used for a given weapon are displayed as
"#d#". The first number indicates how many dice to roll, and the second
indicates how many sides they have. A "2d6" weapon will give damage
from 2 to 12, plus any damage bonus. The weight of a weapon is also a
consideration. Be aware that attacking with a heavier weapon raises the
chance of scoring an especially deadly blow (a critical hit). Depending
on your strength, dexterity, class and weapon weight, you may get
several attacks per turn.


***** <MissileLaunch>
=== Missile Launchers ===

Ammunition (arrows, bullets and so on) shows its full power when it is
fired from a launcher suited to it. You may of course throw an arrow at
a monster without firing it from a bow, but you will find the effects
may not be what you had hoped. Guns fire bullets, bows fire arrows and
crossbows fire bolts. Missiles of varying type and quality can be
bought in the town and may be found throughout the dungeon.

A launcher for attacking from a distance (a bow, etc.) boosts the
capabilities of the arrows or bullets it fires. If the proper
launcher/missile combination is used, the launcher's multiplier is
applied to the total damage, making missile weapons very powerful given
the proper missiles. This is especially true if the missiles are
enchanted.

Hits and misses are determined by the attacker's skill versus the
defender's armor class (AC). A hit is a strike that does some damage;
a miss does no damage, of course. A strike that hits but does no damage
at all is also treated as a miss. Higher armor class makes it harder to
do damage, and so leads to more misses; it will also reduce the damage
from a strike that actually occurs.

The varying types of missile launchers each have their own strengths
and weaknesses, and have different multipliers and numbers of shots.
The number of shots is the number of times you can fire in one turn's
worth of time. They can be summarized as follows:

                    Shots per turn    Multiplier
Pistol:                  1.25             2
Assault Rifle:           0.83             2
Sniper Rifle:            0.83             4
Shotgun:                 0.33             2
Rocket Launcher:         0.67             -
Short Bow:               1.00             2
Long Bow:                1.00             3
Bowgun:                  0.83             3
Crossbow:                0.75             4


Besides the usual description, the missiles that can be fired from your
current launcher get a "(#/#)" display added. These show, respectively,
the average damage of one shot and the average damage converted to one
turn's worth of time. In general, a launcher with a larger second
number is the more powerful weapon.

Bows tend to be good at dealing constant streams of damage. Crossbows
deal enormous amounts of damage in one shot, but it takes time to load
a bolt, so a long bow will deal more damage over time. A pistol is
good for quickly killing weak enemies. An assault rifle fires three
rounds per shot and delivers powerful fire. A sniper rifle can fire a
carefully aimed single shot at a target, but it takes time. A shotgun
is a scattergun that fires many pellets around the target with one
shot and has incredible power, but loading the shells takes even longer
than a crossbow. A rocket launcher fires rockets that cause an
explosion, so it is suited to area attacks.

Except for firearms, a launcher's multiplier is increased further if
your strength is high. Also, raising your skill level with a launcher
increases the number of shots per turn you get with it.


***** <EgoArtifact>
=== Ego Weapons and Artifacts ===

In addition to the ordinary weapons your character may find in the
dungeon, some of them may be endowed with additional powers. These
weapons fall into two types: (1) artifacts; and (2) ego weapons. Unlike
artifacts, which are unique and may only be found once in each game, it
is not unusual to find several ego weapons of the same type during the
course of a character's adventures.

In general, artifacts and ego weapons may boost one or more of your
primary statistics, may confer certain abilities upon your character,
may grant resistance to certain forms of attack and may be especially
deadly against certain types of creature. Take note that if your weapon
has two attributes that make it deadly to your opponent (for example
you are fighting a demon and your weapon slays both evil and demons
(demons are evil)), only the modifier with the larger multiplier will
apply.

(Defender)
    A magical weapon that actually helps defend the wielder by
    increasing his/her armor class and providing resistance against
    damage from fire, cold, acid and lightning attacks. It also grants
    levitation, lets you see invisible creatures, increases your
    stealth and protects you from paralyzation and some slowing
    attacks. It also helps you regenerate hit points and mana faster.
    It has a randomly chosen ability to sustain a statistic and to
    provide additional resistance. Some of them also give warnings to
    the wielder.

(Holy Avenger)
    A Holy Avenger is one of the more powerful of weapons. It will
    increase your wisdom and your armor class and prevent you from
    becoming afraid. This weapon will do extra damage when used against
    evil, demonic and undead creatures, and lets you see invisible
    creatures. It is blessed and so can be wielded by priests with no
    penalty. It has a randomly chosen
    ability to sustain a statistic, and some of those found at very
    deep levels of the dungeon have the very powerful ability to
    increase your number of attacks.

Weapon of Lodis
    A Weapon of Lodis is one of the more powerful weapons. It
    increases the wielder's constitution, allows you to see invisible
    creatures and protects you from paralyzation and some slowing
    attacks. Each one is also given, at random, one of the following:
    blessing with slay evil; unholiness with slay good; or one to three
    slays chosen from animals, undead, demons, orcs, trolls, giants,
    dragons, humans and living creatures.

(Asmode)
    This weapon, imbued with the power of Asmode, the god of darkness,
    does great damage to good and living enemies and increases your
    ability to find traps and doors hidden in the dungeon. It also
    helps you regenerate hit points and mana faster. In addition, it
    grants resistance to darkness and protects you from paralyzation
    and some slowing attacks. It may also have an additional
    resistance or ability.

(Ishtalle)
    This weapon, imbued with the power of Ishtalle, the god of light,
    increases your strength. It also does great damage to evil
    monsters, undead, demons and the like. It also allows you to see
    invisible creatures and protects you from paralyzation and some
    slowing attacks. It has an additional, random, resistance.

(Blessed)
    A blessed weapon will increase your wisdom and can be wielded by
    priests with no penalty. It may have some other special abilities.
    In addition, unless the opponent is a good monster, it can ignore
    that monster's weapon resistance.

Weapon of Extra Attacks
    These weapons will grant the user additional attacks per turn.

Weapon of Sharpness (swords only)
    These are known to often score vorpal hits (see below) and will
    also increase your ability to tunnel through the dungeon walls.

Weapon of Earthquakes (hafted weapons only)
    These weapons may cause an earthquake when they strike an opponent
    or when activated with the activate command. When that happens,
    other monsters nearby may take damage from falling rocks, and a
    small area of the dungeon around the player will collapse. They
    also increase your ability to tunnel through the dungeon walls and
    may stun your opponent.

Weapon of Slaying
    These weapons have unusually large damage dice. Some weapons of
    this type deal poison damage or cause vorpal hits.

(Dragoon)
    Effective against dragons, it inflicts damage with 5 times the
    normal dice against them. It also gives some resistance.

Weapon of Ripper (not hafted weapons)
    These weapons often score vorpal hits and are especially effective
    against human-type opponents. Some of them are also strong against
    particular races.

Digging implements of Exeter, Barmamutha and Basque
    These digging tools are just the thing for tunneling through the
    dungeon walls. An implement of Exeter has one more damage die than
    normal and an improved digging ability. Barmamutha has two more
    dice, an even better digging ability and bonuses to hit and to
    damage; it is a fifth heavier than normal. Basque has three more
    dice, the best digging ability and larger bonuses to hit and to
    damage; it is half again as heavy as normal. None of them can be
    damaged by acid, fire, cold or electricity.


--- The Elemental and Other Brands ---

(Chaotic)
    These bizarre, feared weapons have been warped by Chaos, and the
    effect when they strike an opponent is hard to predict. Effects
    include collapsing the surrounding dungeon, teleporting the
    opponent away, draining the opponent's hit points, and confusing or
    polymorphing the opponent, among others. A Chaotic weapon grants
    resistance to chaos attacks and cannot be damaged by acid, fire or
    electricity. It also has an additional, random, resistance.

(Vampiric)
    These foul weapons have been created by dark magic. They lust for
    blood, and when they strike an enemy they greedily suck its
    life away, transferring that energy to the wielder and healing
    him or her in the process. Some of these weapons are especially
    effective against human-type opponents, dealing 2.5 times the
    normal dice against those creatures.

Weapon of Melting
    A magical weapon of acid that will inflict damage with 2.5 times
    the normal dice when used against a creature that is not resistant
    to acid. It also provides resistance against acid attacks.

Weapon of Shocking
    A magical weapon of lightning that will inflict damage with 2.5
    times the normal dice when used against a creature that is not
    resistant to electricity. It also provides resistance against
    electricity attacks.

Weapon of Freezing
    A magical weapon of ice that will inflict damage with 2.5 times the
    normal dice when used against a creature that is not resistant to
    cold. It also provides resistance against cold attacks.

Weapon of Burning
    A magical weapon of fire that will inflict damage with 2.5 times
    the normal dice when used against a creature that is not resistant
    to fire. It also provides resistance against fire attacks.

Weapon of Venom
    A magical weapon of poison that will inflict damage with 2.5 times
    the normal dice when used against a creature that is not resistant
    to poison. It also increases your stealth and often scores vorpal
    hits.

Weapon of Force
    This weapon uses up your mana when it hits (as long as you have
    some left) and then inflicts damage with 3.5 times the normal dice
    on monsters; if another multiplier (a slay or a brand) also
    applies, that multiplier is raised to one and a half times its
    value plus 2. It also increases your intelligence and allows you
    to see invisible creatures.


--- Weapons of Slay {Monster-Type} ---

Weapon of *Slay* Animal
    This weapon is especially effective against natural creatures and
    will inflict damage with 4 times the normal dice against such
    creatures. It also increases your intelligence and helps you
    regenerate hit points and mana faster.

Weapon of *Slay* Evil
    This weapon is especially effective against evil creatures and
    will inflict damage with 3.5 times the normal dice against such
    creatures. It is blessed, increases your wisdom and provides
    resistance to fear.

Weapon of *Slay* Human
    This weapon is especially effective against human-type creatures
    and will inflict damage with 4 times the normal dice against such
    creatures. It also increases your dexterity.

Weapon of *Slay* Undead
    This weapon is especially effective against undead creatures and
    will inflict damage with 5 times the normal dice against such
    creatures. It is blessed, increases your wisdom, allows you to see
    invisible creatures and provides resistance to life-draining
    attacks (hold life) and to nether.

Weapon of *Slay* Demon
    This weapon is especially effective against demonic creatures and
    will inflict damage with 5 times the normal dice against such
    creatures. It is blessed and increases your intelligence.

Weapon of *Slay* Orc
    This weapon is especially effective against orcs and will inflict
    damage with 5 times the normal dice against such creatures. It also
    increases your dexterity.

Weapon of *Slay* Troll
    This weapon is especially effective against trolls and will inflict
    damage with 5 times the normal dice against such creatures. It also
    increases your strength.

Weapon of *Slay* Giant
    This weapon is especially effective against giants and will inflict
    damage with 5 times the normal dice against such creatures. It also
    increases your strength.

Weapon of *Slay* Good
    This weapon is especially effective against good creatures and
    will inflict damage with 3.5 times the normal dice against such
    creatures. It is unholy, increases your intelligence and provides
    resistance to fear.

Weapon of *Slay* Living
    This weapon is especially effective against living creatures and
    will inflict damage with 3.5 times the normal dice against such
    creatures. It is unholy and increases your constitution.

(There is no ego weapon dedicated to slaying dragons in general; the
(Dragoon) weapons described above are the ones aimed at dragons.)


--- Missile Launchers ---

Launcher of Accuracy
    These missile launchers have an unusually high bonus to hit.

Launcher of Velocity
    These missile launchers have an unusually high bonus to damage.

Launcher of Extra Might
    These missile launchers have a higher launcher multiplier than
    normal. They also provide some resistance.

Launcher of Extra Shots
    These missile launchers can fire more shots per turn than normal.

--- Ammunition ---

Ammunition of Hurt Animal
    This ammunition is especially effective against natural creatures
    and will do 1.7 times the normal damage against such creatures.

Ammunition of Hurt Evil
    This ammunition is especially effective against evil creatures and
    will do 1.5 times the normal damage against such creatures.

Ammunition of Hurt Dragon
    This ammunition is especially effective against dragons and will do
    2 times the normal damage against such creatures.

Ammunition of Shocking
    This ammunition will inflict 1.7 times the normal damage when
    used against a creature that is not resistant to electricity.

Ammunition of Flame
    This ammunition will inflict 1.7 times the normal damage when
    used against a creature that is not resistant to fire.

Ammunition of Frost
    This ammunition will inflict 1.7 times the normal damage when
    used against a creature that is not resistant to cold.

Ammunition of Slaying
    This ammunition has unusually large damage dice.

Ammunition of Wounding
    This ammunition has unusually large bonuses to hit and to damage.


--- Other Items ---

Apart from these there are some rare and powerful weapons which are not
ego items. Maces of Disruption do great damage to undead creatures,
and Diamond Edges may score vorpal hits.

Also, legendary weapons (artifacts) are similar to ego items but
usually have more abilities and are very powerful. Artifacts never
break. However, they may deteriorate and, if caught in the effect
range of *Destruction*, may disappear forever.

In the world of TOband, there are two types of artifacts: fixed and
random. The game itself makes no distinction between the two, but a
fixed artifact has a predetermined name and attributes, so, if you
play the game many times, you may encounter the same fixed artifact.
The name and attributes of a random artifact are determined when it is
generated, so it is extremely unlikely that you would see that
artifact again in another game.

By the way, the rare weapons mentioned above can also become ego
items at the same time. A Diamond Edge (Holy Avenger) will have
a more powerful attack than many artifacts.


Note on Vorpal Weapons: A weapon with the vorpal flag will have a
1-in-6 chance of doing additional damage each time it strikes. If it
passes the roll it has a chance of doing it again. This continues until
a roll is failed. The calculations are nasty but the net effect is an
average 22% increase in damage output.


***** <MagicalAids>
=== Magical Aids to Physical Combat ===

There are several magical means of increasing your physical combat
ability. The most common of these are potions of Heroism and Berserk
Strength and various scrolls (Blessing, Holy Prayer, etc.). These
grant small bonuses to your combat skill, and the bonuses are
cumulative. Some magic realms contain equivalent spells. Be aware that
you can't cast spells while berserk from a potion of Berserk Strength.
If you really want to cast a spell while berserk, drinking a potion of
Cure Light Wounds or the like will clear your head.


***** <BareHandAttacks>
=== Bare-Handed Attacks ===

A TOband character can also fight bare-handed. As your level and your
Martial Arts skill rise, the number of attacks you get per turn
increases and your techniques become increasingly powerful. Higher
level attacks have a chance to stun your opponent.

While the type of attack used for each blow is chosen at random from
the list of available techniques, as your Martial Arts skill level
rises there is a bias towards the techniques which do greater damage.
This is because the game rolls several times for each blow and the
highest-level technique is the one chosen.

***** <MeleeTactics>
=== Basic Tactics ===


Pillardancing
-------------
This is the classic tactic for when you are faster than your enemy:
find an isolated wall surrounded by eight floor squares, lure the
enemy to it, and when you and the enemy are next to the wall, attack
it, then run to the opposite side of the wall from it, and repeat the
whole thing from the start.

In TOband, however, this method is of no use at all!!! Monsters in
TOband inherently move at erratic speeds. A monster may stand still for
a while and then suddenly move many steps in a row and attack you.


Shoot'n Scoot
-------------
Requires a large room, Phase Door, and some type of ranged attack.
Stand at one end of the room while your enemy is at the other end.
Repeat your ranged attack until it gets next to you, then use Phase
Door to jump to a distant spot. Keep doing that. By the time you run
out of arrows or sling pebbles, the enemy will be dead or nearly so.


Wail'n Bail
-----------
Requires Teleport items. Fight the monster until you're almost dead,
then teleport out. Then find it again and resume fighting. This is
dangerous, because you could teleport right next to some other nasty
monster. Also, it is not generally useful for killing unique monsters,
as they regenerate damage very quickly, and by the time you find them
again, they will have healed what you did to them.


The Anti-Summoning Corridor
---------------------------
Requires a little time to set up. This can be done just about anywhere.
Dig a twisting corridor into the rock, and station yourself at one end
of it. When your opponent arrives, it won't be able to summon any
monsters next to you. This is a very important technique for fighting
powerful monsters which can summon a horde of other monsters.
