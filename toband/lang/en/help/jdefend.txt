=== Defending and Armor ===

In TOband, monsters may damage and otherwise harm you in a number of
ways: in direct melee combat, by exploding in an adjacent square, by
breathing on you and by casting spells. Some monsters referred to as
"summoners" may not directly harm you but may summon other monsters to
do their dirty work for them.

Monsters attack you in the same manner in which you attack them. If
they move into your square, they attack you. Virtually all monsters can
lower your hit points by means of a melee attack, and, if you lose too
many, you die. Fortunately, this is also the attack most easily guarded
against (by wearing armor). Each monster has a maximum of four melee
attacks per turn, which may be of varying type, power and effect.

In addition to pure damage, there can be a number of side effects from
a monster hitting you. For example, an especially powerful attack may
stun or wound you. Stunning is cumulative and, if not addressed, grows
worse step by step and may eventually result in you becoming knocked
out, which allows your opponent to keep attacking while you lie
helpless. Wounds will cause you to lose hit points each turn until you
either die or your wound heals. You will slowly recover from any wound
except a Mortal Wound with time, and all wounds can be healed
magically.

In addition to wounds and stunning, some monsters' melee attacks may
make you blind, scared, confused, poisoned, paralyzed or petrified.
There are also many other, less common effects. More information about
monsters' melee attacks may be found in the section on Monsters.

Many monsters also have an array of magical spells or breath attacks
which they may use against you from a distance. For example, a red
dragon might breathe fire at you. You can defend yourself from the
side effects of melee attacks, monster spells and breath attacks by
searching for and wearing armor that is resistant to that form of
attack.

As you descend deeper into the dungeon in search of better items,
you will need to steadily improve your defenses against pure damage,
wounds, stunning, bolts, balls, breaths of various kinds, reductions
of exp and stats, theft of items and a variety of miscellaneous
magical attacks.

***** <Armor>
=== Armor Class ===

Your armor class (or AC) is a number that describes the total defensive
value of the armor you are wearing. Armor class will generally run from
about 0 to 150, but could become negative or greater than 150 with
rarer armor or by magical means. Note that spell casters receive a
penalty to their maximum mana for wearing armor that is too heavy.

The larger your armor class, the more protective it is. A negative
armor class would actually help get you hit; it makes you easier to hit
than if you wore nothing at all. Armor protects you in three ways.
First, it makes you harder to hit for damage. A hit for no damage is
treated the same as a miss. Second, a high armor class reduces the
damage that your character would have taken from normal attacks.
Third, acid damage is reduced by wearing body armor (but the armor will
be damaged instead). It is obvious that a high armor class is a must
for surviving the deeper levels of the TOband dungeon. Note, however,
that armor class by itself will not protect you from the side effects
of monster melee attacks mentioned above.

Each piece of armor has a base armor value, which, like the damage from
weapons, is assumed known by the player and is obvious as soon as you
pick it up. The magical bonus, however, which enhances the base armor
class, will not be displayed unless the armor has been identified or
was bought in a store.

Armor class values are always displayed between a set of brackets as
"[#]" or "[#,+#]". The first value is the armor class of the item. The
second number is the magical bonus of the item, which is only displayed
if known and will always have a sign preceding the value.

Note: a few rings, amulets and weapons also have the "[+#]" notation,
indicating that they provide an armor bonus. Many pieces of heavy body
armor will also have a "(-#)" before the "[#,+#]", which indicates that
the weight of the armor decreases your chances of hitting monsters.
This is not displayed for very light armor, since its effect is
negligible, and can reach (-8) for the very heaviest armor!


***** <ResistingMagic>
=== Resisting Magical Attacks ===

Against some magical attacks (such as cause wounds, cause blindness or
cause fear) the player will first get a saving throw (see the Skills
page). However, against attacks such as bolts and ball spells this does
not apply. Also, there is no saving throw against breath attacks. For
these types of attacks, your only recourse is to find items or other
means which grant you "resistance" to that type of attack.

In TOband there are many forms of resistance, which may be obtained
either as bonuses from your equipment, as the result of casting a
magical spell, activating certain artifacts or as the result of a
mutation. Unfortunately, not all attacks available to monsters can be
resisted, and most can only be partially resisted.

Each resistance has one or more corresponding monster attacks which it
"resists". By this we mean that it reduces the amount of damage that
your character will take as a result of an attack of that kind. For
example, fire resistance will reduce the damage to your character
resulting from fire attacks such as a fire bolt or being breathed upon
by a red dragon. Note that appropriate resistances will also negate or
reduce some side effects of monster melee attacks.

There are two categories of resistances: (1) the low (or elemental)
resistances - acid, fire, cold and electricity; and (2) the high
resistances - poison, confusion, light, dark, blindness, sound, fear,
shards, stone, chaos, nether and disenchantment. In general, the low
resistances are more commonly available, they can be double-resisted
(see below) and the corresponding attacks have a higher maximum damage
potential.

A common misconception amongst new players is that having multiple
sources of a particular resistance will increase your character's
resistance to attacks of that form. This is not true. You will get the
same benefit from wearing a single Amulet of Resist Acid as from
wearing a Shield of Resist Acid, body armor that grants acid
resistance and an artifact that grants acid resistance, all at the same
time.

As stated above, however, it is possible to get double resistance for
the low resists (and also poison). To get double resistance, you must
first have a permanent resistance from an item or the like, and then
add a temporary resistance on top of it, by quaffing a Potion of
Resistance, casting a spell that grants resistance to everything, etc.
With such double resistance, the damage you take from the corresponding
attack is dramatically reduced, and your belongings are also protected
from being destroyed by fire, cold, electricity and acid.

(Warning 1) Double resistance prevents an item from being destroyed,
but it does not prevent equipment from being damaged by acid. It is
necessary to obtain acid immunity to prevent that form of damage.

(Warning 2) The magical spells in some realms can temporarily add a
high resistance, like nether, in addition to the five lower resistances
(the four basic elements and poison). That kind of temporary
resistance does not combine with a permanent resistance to become a
double resistance.


***** <EgoArtifact>
=== Ego Armor and Artifacts ===

In addition to the ordinary armor items your character may find in
the dungeon, your character may find armor endowed with additional
powers. Those fall into two types: (1) artifacts and (2) ego armor.
Unlike artifacts, which are unique and may only be found once in each
game, it is not unusual to find several pieces of ego armor of the
same type during the course of a character's adventures.

In general, artifacts and ego armor may boost one or more of your
primary statistics, may confer certain abilities upon your character
and may grant resistance to certain forms of attack. Each ego type
may only be found on certain types of armor - for example, you can
find Armor of Zenobian but not Boots of Zenobian.


--- Armor/Shields ---

of Resist Acid
     A character wearing this item will take only one third the normal
     damage from acid. In addition, the item itself is not damaged by
     acid.

of Resist Lightning
     A character wearing this item will take only one third the normal
     damage from electricity.

of Resist Fire
     A character wearing this item will take only one third the normal
     damage from fire.

of Resist Cold
     A character wearing this item will take only one third the normal
     damage from cold.

of Resistance
     A character wearing this item gains resistance to all of acid,
     cold, fire and electricity, and the item itself is not damaged by
     them. Body armor of this type may also have a random additional
     high resistance, such as resistance to poison; shields of this
     type do not.

of Zenobian
     This is almost the same as armor of Resistance, but of finer
     quality. It also enhances the stealth of the wearer. It may also
     have a random additional high resistance, such as resistance to
     poison.

of Gamp
     This armor has a larger bonus to AC than normal armor. It also
     increases the strength of the wearer, and cannot be damaged by
     acid, fire, cold or electricity.

Robe of Permanence
     This robe provides resistance to fire, cold, acid and
     electricity, and cannot be damaged by acid. It also provides
     resistance to experience draining attacks and sustains all six
     primary statistics of the wearer. It may also have a random
     additional high resistance, such as resistance to poison.

Shield of Reflection
     This shield bounces back missiles and bolt spells, which not only
     prevents the damage but also often damages the attacker in turn.
     The bounce effect happens most, but not all, of the time.

Shield of Night and Day
     This shield reduces the damage from light and darkness attacks and
     prevents the blinding effect of those attacks.


--- Caps/Helms/Crowns ---

of the Magi
     This item increases your intelligence, prevents it from being
     reduced, and gives resistance to fire, cold, acid and electricity.
     It also has an additional, random, resistance or ability.

of Might
     This item increases your strength and prevents your strength,
     dexterity and constitution from being reduced. It also negates
     paralyzation and some slowing attacks. It has an additional,
     random, resistance.

of Lordliness
     This item increases your wisdom and prevents your wisdom and
     charisma from being reduced. It has an additional, random,
     resistance.

of Seeing
     This item grants the ability to see invisible creatures and
     increases your ability to find traps and secret doors. In
     addition, it prevents you from being blinded.

of Infravision
     This item increases the range of your infravision and grants
     infravision if you are a race which does not have it
     intrinsically.

of Light
     This item provides a permanent light source. It also provides
     resistance to light attacks.

of Telepathy
     This item grants its wearer telepathy.

of Regeneration
     This item helps you regenerate hit points and mana more quickly
     than normal, allowing you to fight longer before needing to rest.
     You will use food faster than normal while wearing it.

--- Cloaks ---

of Protection
     These cloaks cannot be harmed by acid attacks and grant
     resistance to shards. They have a larger than normal bonus to AC.

of Guardian
     These cloaks cannot be harmed by acid, fire, cold or lightning
     attacks but do not grant resistance to those elements.

of Stealth
     These cloaks make your character more stealthy.

of Arcadia
     These cloaks cannot be harmed by acid, fire, cold or lightning
     attacks but do not grant resistance to those elements. They also
     make your character more stealthy and provide an additional high
     resistance.

of Immolation
     These cloaks cannot be harmed by acid or fire attacks. They also
     grant resistance to fire and give out a fiery aura.

of Electricity
     These cloaks cannot be harmed by acid or electricity attacks. They
     also grant resistance to electricity and give out an electric
     aura.

of the Bat
     These very powerful cloaks grant a large stealth bonus,
     levitation, the ability to see invisible creatures and resistance
     to darkness. However, in return for those abilities, they carry
     penalties to your to-hit, to-damage and AC.


--- Gloves/Gauntlets/Sets of Cesti ---

of Free Action
     This item negates paralyzation and slowing attacks. A mage-type
     spellcaster who wears them will not have his or her mana
     penalized.

of Slaying
     In addition to its base AC bonus, this item grants a bonus to
     your to-hit and to-damage.


--- Boots ---

of Levitation
     These boots grant levitation.

of Stealth
     These boots make your character more stealthy.

of Free Action
     These boots negate paralyzation.

of Dimension Door
     These boots can be activated to cast a dimension door, which
     instantly teleports you to a spot of your choice a short distance
     away, once every 50+d25 turns.

of Speed
     These boots make your character move faster.


--- Special items that are not ego items ---

Dragon Scale Mail
     These extremely rare pieces of armor come in many different
     colors, and each gives resistance to the element of the dragons of
     that color. They cannot be damaged by acid, fire, cold or
     electricity. They also have the ability to breathe as a dragon
     would!

Dragon Shield
Dragon Helm
Dragon Boots
Dragon Gloves
     These items have a chance of granting one or more random
     resistances. They cannot be damaged by acid, fire, cold or
     electricity.

--- Other items ---


Apart from these there are some very rare, and well made, pieces of
armor in the dungeon. Mithril Plate Mail and Mithril Chain Mail are
not damaged by acid because of the quality metals they contain. Mirror
Shields have the ability to reflect bolt spells, Bolmarkan Cloaks grant
the wearer free action, resistance to fire and extra dexterity (and
cannot be damaged by acid, fire, cold or lightning), and Shadow Cloaks
provide resistance to darkness and a large stealth bonus. Raincoats
provide resistance to water, a very special resistance. All of those
could also be ego items or artifacts.

There are also legendary pieces of armor (artifacts), which are similar
to ego items but usually have more abilities and are very powerful.
Artifacts never break. However, they may deteriorate and, if caught
in the effect range of *Destruction*, may disappear forever.

In the world of TOband, there are two types of artifacts: fixed and
random. The game itself makes no distinction between the two, but a
fixed artifact has a predetermined name and attributes, so, if you
play the game many times, you may encounter the same fixed artifact.
The name and attributes of a random artifact are determined when it is
generated, so it is extremely unlikely that you would see that
artifact again in another game.



***** <Resistances>
=== The Resistances ===

This section describes the various types of attack available to
monsters and how they may be resisted.

Acid
    Acid resistance will cut damage from acid attacks by two-thirds.
    Temporary resistance will also reduce damage by two-thirds. This
    means that a character with both permanent and temporary
    resistance will only take one-ninth of the original damage. Double
    resistance prevents the destruction of acid-sensitive belongings.
    Acid resistance also protects you from instant petrification by
    stone attacks (see Stone below).

Fire
    Fire resistance will cut damage from fire attacks by two-thirds.
    Temporary resistance will also reduce damage by two-thirds. This
    means that a character with both permanent and temporary
    resistance will only take one-ninth of the original damage. Fire
    resistance helps protect equipment from fire damage; double
    resistance prevents the destruction of fire-sensitive belongings.

Cold
    Cold resistance will cut damage from cold attacks by two-thirds.
    Temporary resistance will also reduce damage by two-thirds. This
    means that a character with both permanent and temporary
    resistance will only take one-ninth of the original damage. Cold
    resistance helps protect equipment from cold damage; double
    resistance prevents the destruction of cold-sensitive belongings.

Electricity
    Electricity resistance will cut damage from electric attacks by
    two-thirds. Temporary resistance will also reduce damage by
    two-thirds. This means that a character with both permanent and
    temporary resistance will only take one-ninth of the original
    damage. Electricity resistance helps protect equipment from damage
    due to electricity; double resistance prevents the destruction of
    electricity-sensitive belongings.

Poison
    Poison resistance will cut damage from poison attacks by
    two-thirds. Temporary resistance will also reduce damage by
    two-thirds. This means that a character with both permanent and
    temporary resistance will only take one-ninth of the original
    damage. Poison resistance will also protect you from becoming
    "poisoned", but a character who is already poisoned will not be
    healed if he or she becomes resistant to poison afterward. That
    must be cured by other means.

Confusion
    Confusion resistance will reduce the damage taken from confusion
    attacks and breaths. It will also prevent your character from
    becoming "Confused" (if you are confused you will be unable to
    read scrolls, cast spells or prayers and will have a dramatically
    reduced ability to use magical devices). In particular, water
    attacks also have confusion as a side effect. That can be
    prevented if you have confusion resistance.

Light
    Light breath attacks damage the player and cause blindness as a
    side effect. Light resistance reduces the damage and also prevents
    blindness caused by light attacks (but not by other attacks).

Darkness
    Darkness breath attacks damage the player and cause blindness as a
    side effect. Darkness resistance reduces the damage and also
    prevents blindness caused by darkness attacks (but not by other
    attacks). In addition, some high-level monsters can cast Darkness
    Storms. Those are a particularly dangerous form of attack, but
    the damage is reduced if you have darkness resistance.

Blindness
    Resist blindness will protect you from spells which blind and from
    attacks that blind (a few monsters can do this), as well as from
    the blindness (but not the damage) caused by light and darkness
    attacks.

Sound
    Sound resistance reduces damage from sound attacks and also
    prevents the stunning side effect of sound and some other attacks.
    (Sound, plasma, water, force, gravity and shards attacks may all
    stun the player, as may ice bolt attacks). Sound resistance does
    not protect against stunning from melee attacks.

Fear
    Fear resistance gives immunity to attacks which make your
    character "Afraid" (being afraid prevents your character from
    fighting in melee but does not otherwise affect you).

Shards
    Shards resistance will reduce the damage taken from shards attacks
    (this includes rocket attacks). It also prevents the cuts that
    come from these attacks (and also from ice bolts). Some shards
    attacks may also stun the player; this is prevented if you have
    sound resistance, not by shards resistance. Shards resistance
    also protects you from instant petrification by stone attacks (see
    Stone below).

Stone
    Stone attacks are among the most threatening attacks in TOband.
    A stone attack damages the player and may petrify him or her at
    the same time. Stone resistance reduces the damage from such
    attacks and fully protects the player from petrification. Without
    it, you first get a saving throw; if the saving throw fails and
    you have neither acid resistance nor shards resistance, you turn
    into a statue on the spot and die. If you do have acid or shards
    resistance, petrification starts but advances only gradually: it
    gets worse every turn, slows you down more and more, and kills you
    when it is complete. Resistances do not slow that progress, and
    petrification that is already in progress must be cured by other
    means.

Chaos
    Chaos attacks are among the most dangerous attacks in TOband. They
    have a relatively high maximum damage and may also confuse the
    player, cause hallucinations, reduce your character's experience
    and cause you to mutate. Chaos resistance will reduce the damage
    taken from such attacks and prevent hallucination, mutation and
    experience loss; the confusion is prevented only by confusion
    resistance. Even without chaos resistance, nether resistance or
    hold life will reduce the experience drained by the side effects.

Nether
    Nether is another dangerous attack type. Nether attacks are common
    deeper in the dungeon, have a high maximum damage and will also
    cause your character's experience to be drained. Nether resistance
    will reduce the damage taken and the experience loss.

Gravity
    Gravity attacks damage the player and also cause you (and the
    monsters they hit) to be teleported a short distance. A side
    effect of this is that, since the effect is calculated on each
    spot in turn, it is possible to be blinked into a spot which
    hasn't been calculated yet but is in line to be affected by the
    breath - the net result is to be hit twice, blinked twice and
    damaged twice by the same breath. Gravity attacks may also slow
    and stun your character. Levitation reduces the damage and
    prevents the slowing and the stunning, resist teleportation
    prevents the blinking around and resistance to sound prevents you
    from becoming stunned.

Disenchantment
    Disenchantment attacks damage the player and may result in the
    to-hit, to-dam, to-AC and pval bonuses on your equipment being
    reduced. Disenchantment resistance will reduce the damage you take
    and will prevent the disenchantment of your equipment.

Toxic Waste
    Poison resistance will reduce the damage and stop the side
    effects. Without poison resistance, you are poisoned, and you may
    suffer mutations or a polymorph, which can deform your body and,
    in the worst case, even swap your sex.


***** <Unresistable>
--- Unresistable Attacks ---

There is no resistance to the following types of attack, although some
of their side effects may be resisted.

Time
    Time attacks damage the player, drain experience and may reduce one
    or more of your primary statistics. Some kinds of magic or very
    rare items grant resistance to time attacks, but you usually won't
    have any resistance.

Plasma
    Plasma can damage you, stun you and burn your belongings. Fire
    resistance (or immunity) prevents the burning of belongings, sound
    resistance prevents the stunning, but there is no resistance to
    the damage.

Inertia
    Inertia will damage you and slow you down by -10 speed. There is
    no resistance to the damage or the slowing.

Mana
    There is no resistance to this attack. It also destroys all items
    lying on the floor.

Disintegration
    There is no resistance to this attack. It also destroys all walls
    and items within its area of effect (except permanent rock).

*Fire*
    This is pure elemental energy, so pure that it breaks through every
    kind of defense. Manipulating the terrain, the element of the
    attacker and your own element is the only way to reduce the
    damage. Double resistance or immunity to fire merely prevents
    fire-sensitive belongings from being destroyed. All items lying on
    the floor are destroyed.

*Aqua*
    This is pure elemental energy, so pure that it breaks through every
    kind of defense. Manipulating the terrain, the element of the
    attacker and your own element is the only way to reduce the
    damage. Double resistance or immunity to cold merely prevents
    cold-sensitive belongings from being destroyed. All items lying on
    the floor are destroyed.

*Earth*
    This is pure elemental energy, so pure that it breaks through every
    kind of defense. Manipulating the terrain, the element of the
    attacker and your own element is the only way to reduce the
    damage. Double resistance or immunity to acid merely prevents
    acid-sensitive belongings from being destroyed. All items lying on
    the floor are destroyed.

*Wind*
    This is pure elemental energy, so pure that it breaks through every
    kind of defense. Manipulating the terrain, the element of the
    attacker and your own element is the only way to reduce the
    damage. Double resistance or immunity to electricity merely
    prevents electricity-sensitive belongings from being destroyed.
    All items lying on the floor are destroyed.


***** <Immunities>
--- Immunities ---

It is also possible that you may find items which grant immunity to
one or (very occasionally) more of the low resistances, i.e. acid,
fire, cold and electricity. In this case, when worn, you will take no
damage from attacks of the relevant kind and, in addition, your
equipment and inventory are safe from being damaged or destroyed by
the attack.

In addition, free action, fear resistance and blindness resistance may
be thought of as immunities, since they protect you totally from the
respective form of attack.

(Warning) Even if you have free action, you may be paralyzed in
special circumstances. This can happen when you are hungry, or when you
cast a spell without having enough mana.


***** <Miscellaneous>
--- Miscellaneous ---

The following are not properly resistances but may be sensibly
included on this page.

Free Action
    Free action is immunity to paralysis. This is foolproof except in
    the special case of the Ancient and Foul Curse, which requires a
    successful saving throw as well as free action to avoid should it
    attempt to paralyze you. Paralyzing spells, potions of paralysis
    and attacks that hit to paralyze are completely protected against.

Hold-life
    Hold Life is protection from experience draining. It is not
    foolproof: depending on the attack, the chance that it holds is
    between 50% and 95% (75% against nether attacks). When it fails,
    you are still drained, but by much less than would otherwise have
    been the case.


***** <Recovering>
=== Recovering from Attacks ===

You cannot fight the monsters in TOband and expect to come out
unscathed every time. When a monster inflicts damage on you, you will
need to take steps to recover from the damage as soon as possible.

--- Healing Items ---

Healing is available in multiple forms including, from weakest to
strongest: Potions and Staffs of Cure Light Wounds, Potions of Cure
Critical Wounds, Potions, Staffs and Rods of Healing, Potions of
*Healing* and Potions of Life. Some magic realms, such as Holy, also
contain spells to heal the player.

Cure Light Wounds, Cure Serious Wounds, Cure Critical Wounds
     Cure Light Wounds restores 2d8 hit points, reduces cuts and
     removes blindness and berserk.
     Cure Serious Wounds restores 4d8 hit points, reduces cuts and
     removes blindness, confusion and berserk.
     Cure Critical Wounds restores 6d8 hit points and removes cuts,
     blindness, confusion, stun, poison and berserk.

Curing
     This potion focuses more on curing various status ailments than
     on healing wounds. It removes cuts, blindness, confusion, stun,
     poison and hallucination, and also restores 50 hit points.

*Curing*
     Restores 700 hit points and removes cuts, blindness, confusion,
     stun, poison, hallucination and gradually progressing
     petrification.

Healing
     Restores 300 hit points and removes cuts, blindness, confusion,
     stun, poison and berserk.

*Healing*
     Restores 1200 hit points and removes cuts, blindness, confusion,
     stun, poison and berserk.

Potions of Life
     Cures basically everything bad; restores 5000 hit points, restores
     all drained stats and experience, and removes all temporary bad
     effects. These are very rare; save them in your home for a
     dangerous fight.


--- Restoring Items ---

From time to time your primary statistics and/or experience may be
drained. Statistics can be restored by quaffing a Potion of Restore
{name of stat} or a Potion of Restoring. Some towns have a building
that offers stat restoration services for a fee. Experience may be
restored by quaffing a Potion of Restore Life Levels or using a Rod of
Restoration. You may also regain it by defeating enemies or by
quaffing a Potion of Experience to earn it again, but part of the
experience gained that way will be wasted. Some spells regain lost
experience points.

--- Other ---

There are also other items in the dungeon which may help you to recover
from various things that monsters may do to you. For example, a Potion
of Heroism will prevent you from becoming afraid, a Potion of Slow
Poison will reduce the effects of being poisoned, etc. Most objects'
names give a clear idea of the benefits and dangers of using them, so
an exhaustive list is not provided in this document.
