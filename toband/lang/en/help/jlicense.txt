----------------------
About the dual license
----------------------
At the suggestion of Robert Ruehlmann, an effort has been under way to make
Angband and its variants distributable under the GPL. For details see the page
about the Angband OpenSource Initiative on the official Angband home page.

http://thangorodrim.angband.org/development/opensource.html

Translation (partly paraphrased) of that page:

Angband and its predecessor Moria were written before the idea of OpenSource
was widely accepted, but their authors made a license guaranteeing that
Angband and Moria would always be distributed free of charge.
(Translator's note: this is the so-called Moria/Angband license.)

Copyright (c) 1997 Ben Harrison, James E. Wilson, Robert A. Koeneke

This software may be copied and distributed for educational, research,
and not for profit purposes provided that this copyright and statement
are included in all such copies.  Other copyrights may also apply.

That license, however, does not meet the conditions of OpenSource: there is
no obligation to publish the source when distributing a changed version, and
there were several other problems besides. As a result Angband cannot lawfully
be included in a Linux distribution, nor can it use the many services prepared
for OpenSource projects (SourceForge and the like), nor can code distributed
under the GPL be used as part of Angband.

I (Robert Ruehlmann) have begun the Angband OpenSource Initiative to
overcome these problems, and am working to make it possible to distribute
Angband (and any variant that wishes to share in the benefit of the license
change) under the GNU General Public License.

Changing the license requires either the agreement of everyone who wrote any
of the code in Angband, or else a complete rewrite of every part written by an
author who has not agreed to the new license.

Moving Angband to the GPL alone would force the authors of every variant based
on Angband to change their license to the GPL as well. To avoid that, I have
decided to use the "dual license" method, distributing the code under both the
GPL and the Angband license. Variant authors may therefore go on using the
Angband license if they wish; they need not change their variant's license to
the GPL.

Note that at present the dual license applies only to the parts of the source
written by those who agreed to the Angband OpenSource Initiative. Other parts
are still distributed under the Moria/Angband license alone, so the dual
license does not affect the whole of Angband as it is distributed today. Until
every part of Angband can be distributed under both licenses, the only license
valid for the program as a whole is the Moria/Angband license.

To distribute your own changes under the dual license, you may use the short
statement below and send it to me by mail.
