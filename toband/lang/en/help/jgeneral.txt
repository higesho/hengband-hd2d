=== General Information ===

This document gives a brief introduction to TOband. Before beginning any
serious adventuring, you will probably want to browse through all of the
help files, especially the sections on available commands (see jcommand.txt
[b] and jcommdesc.txt [c]).

The game of TOband is a single-player dungeon simulation. A player may
choose from a number of races and classes (see jbirth.txt [d]) when creating
a character, and then control that character in the dungeon over a period of
days, weeks, even months. Deep down inside, the real objective of the game is
to gain experience and certain other characteristics, and to collect useful
items, so that you become strong enough to defeat the great Overlord
'Dorgalua, the King', who lurks in the depths of the dungeon.


***** <First>
=== Your First Steps ===

The player begins his or her adventure in town. There the player acquires
various items, weapons, armor and magical goods by buying and selling with
the various shopkeepers. Then the player sets foot in the dungeon, where a
wonderful adventure awaits!

Races that live and breathe must carry plenty of food when they leave town.


--- The Global Map ----  ( Just press '<' to see the whole vast world!!! )

Since this world is very large, it is convenient to enter the global map by
pressing '<' when you leave town, and then return to the normal map by
pressing '>' when you reach your destination. In the wilderness, roads and
grasslands are relatively safe, but lava fields and the open sea are home to
powerful monsters and are dangerous. You will have to grow stronger before
you can go to such places.

Many dungeons await brave adventurers, such as the "Palace of the Dead".
The ultimate goal of this game is "Dorgalua, the King", who dwells at the
bottom of the "Ancient Ruins". The way to the Ancient Ruins opens only after
you have conquered the "Hanging Gardens".

For more about the overworld map, see jtown.txt [f].


***** <Winning>
--- Winning The Game ---

On level 99 of the Ancient Ruins lives the Dark Knight 'Lanselot Tartaros'.
(If your class is Templar, it is 'Denam Morne, the Lord' who waits there
instead.) You cannot reach 'Dorgalua, the King', who lurks on level 100 of
the Ancient Ruins, without first defeating the guardian of level 99. Try not
to wander around on level 100 unless you are ready for him.

'Dorgalua, the King' cannot be killed by the easier methods used on normal
monsters. Like all other quest monsters, if you use a spell such as
destruction on his area, he will simply teleport away to another region of
the level. He cannot be polymorphed, slept, charmed or genocided. Spells
such as Mana Storm and Summon Ignis Fatuus are effective against him, as are
some of the more powerful weapons, but he is difficult to kill, and if
allowed to escape for a time he will heal rapidly.

If you actually survive the battle with 'Dorgalua, the King' and kill him,
you will receive the status of WINNER ("You have won the game!"). You may
continue to explore, and may even save the game and play more later.

When you are ready to retire, simply "commit suicide" (SHIFT-Q) to have your
character entered into the high score list as a winner. Note that until you
retire, you can still be killed, so you may want to retire before wandering
into a hoard of Great Wyrms of Power...


***** <UponDeath>
--- Upon Death and Dying ---

If your character falls below 0 hit points, he or she has died and, with a
few exceptions, cannot be restored. You can write your dying words at the
end. A tombstone showing information about your character will then be
displayed. You are also permitted to get a record of your character, and all
of your equipment, either on the screen or in a file.

The character is erased from the save file, which then keeps only the
monster memory (see jmonster.txt [e]) and your option settings. If that file
is restored, the new character is generated exactly as if the file was not
there, but the new character will find its monster memory containing all the
experience of past incarnations.
