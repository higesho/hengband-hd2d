=== Races, Classes, Elements ===

This file describes the races, classes and elements that the player can
choose in TOband. At the end of the file there are tables of the stat and
skill modifiers for each race and class.


***** <TheRaces>
=== The Races ===

TOband has 11 races to choose from. Each race has its own strengths and
weaknesses, and its own modifiers to stats and abilities. Many races also
have innate abilities and powers.


***** <Human>
--- Human ---

    The Human is the baseline character; all other races are compared to
    humans. Humans can take up any class and do about average in every one.
    Because humans have short lifespans, they tend to gain levels faster
    than any other race. They have no special modifiers or abilities.


***** <Hawkman>
--- Hawkman ---

    Hawkmen are a fierce, battle-loving winged race. They are long-lived,
    with three times the lifespan of a human. By using their wings they can
    avoid pits and dangerous terrain.


***** <Lizardman>
--- Lizardman ---

    Lizardmen are an amphibious race with tough scales. The scales give
    them resistance to acid and cold. They are also excellent fighters,
    with a high hit point gain, and are fairly good at disarming traps and
    using magic devices.


***** <Fairy>
--- Faerie ---

    Faeries are very small. They have little wings and can fly over traps
    and dangerous terrain. They love sunlight and resist light, but take
    great damage from darkness. At high levels they can fly faster.


***** <Gremlin>
--- Gremlin ---

    Gremlins are the opposite of faeries. They have wings and can fly over
    traps and dangerous terrain. They love darkness and resist dark and
    nether, but take great damage from light. At high levels they can fly
    faster.


***** <Skeleton>
--- Skeleton ---

    As undead, Skeletons need not worry about poison or life-draining
    attacks. They do not use eyes to perceive objects, so they cannot be
    fooled by invisible things. Their bones resist shards, and at higher
    levels they gain resistance to cold. The effects of potions and food
    work on a Skeleton without passing through its stomach (which it does
    not have), but the potions and food themselves fall straight through
    its jaw and no nourishment can be absorbed. Skeletons do not need food.


***** <Ghost>
--- Ghost ---

    Ghosts are a powerful kind of undead. Being semi-material, they can
    pass through walls, but they are hurt by the density of the wall when
    they do. Like other undead, they cannot have their life force drained,
    can see invisible things, resist poison and cold, and are further immune
    to nether. At sufficiently high levels they gain telepathy. Ghosts can
    become outstanding spellcasters, but their physical attributes are very
    poor. Ghosts do not need food.


***** <Pumpkin-Head>
--- Pumpkinhead ---

    Pumpkinheads are pumpkin ghosts created by the witch Deneb. They bring
    out the cruelty in their enemies, so enemies tend to target them first.
    Pumpkinheads are physically weak, but are immune to paralysis, resist
    chaos and disenchantment, sustain their life force, and gain telepathy
    as they level up. Only males can choose this race.


***** <Goblin>
--- Goblin ---

    Goblins can make good fighters, but cannot be expected to do much with
    magic. They are poor at stealth, and awful at searching, disarming and
    perception. Goblins are ugly, and tend to be asked higher prices when
    shopping. Because they like to live underground, goblins resist
    darkness.


***** <Gorgon>
--- Gorgon ---

    Gorgons are monsters with countless snakes growing in place of hair, and
    a snake's lower body instead of a mermaid's. They are competent at every
    skill, and are far better with bows than other races. They resist
    petrification, and as they level up they can also gain resistance to
    acid, cold, poison, nether and chaos. On top of that, they can use the
    "evil eye" that turns whoever sees it to stone, which makes this a very
    powerful race. Only females can choose this race.


***** <Mermaid>
--- Mermaid ---

    Mermaids are a sea-dwelling race with a human upper body and a fish's
    lower body. They love the water, and can be expected to fight very well
    in it. On land, however, they are awkward, and moving about takes effort.
    They are also excellent singers, and can sing songs that charm monsters.



***** <TheClasses>
=== The Classes ===

TOband has 25 classes, each with its own strengths and weaknesses and its
own modifiers to the character's abilities. Many classes also have unique
abilities. These often depend on level, and some cannot be used until late
in the game.


***** <Soldier>
--- Warrior ---

    The Warrior is the basic class for male characters. It has no special
    class skills and its parameters grow evenly, so if you raise one as a
    Warrior you will end up with a half-baked jack of all trades. It is,
    however, quite useful for straightening out a character who has become
    too specialized.


***** <Knight>
--- Knight ---

    Knights excel in arms and are ambassadors of the chivalric code of
    loyalty. They cannot use magic, but defend better than Warriors and are
    skilled with swords and polearms. They are also fairly good at riding,
    and their attack power when riding and wielding a lance is devastating.


***** <Berserker>
--- Berserker ---

    Berserkers are savage fighters who cut a path to survival with a
    powerful body and superhuman strength. They are good with axes and blunt
    weapons. They cannot use magic and are poor with magic devices, but
    their instincts, honed on the battlefield, are keen, and they excel at
    disarming, perception and searching.


***** <Terror-Knight>
--- Terror Knight ---

    Terror Knights are fearsome knights who proudly lead the spirits of
    everyone they have killed. Their killing power is almost mythical, but
    they have next to no magical ability.


***** <BeastTamer>
--- Beast Tamer ---

    Beast Tamers are warriors who fight alongside monsters. They favor the
    whip, and can cast Symbiotic magic that supports their pets. They are
    also good at riding, and suffer little penalty even when wielding
    weapons ill-suited to riding. The stat needed to cast spells is wisdom.


***** <Sword-Master>
--- Swordmaster ---

    Swordmasters are seekers of the way who polish their souls by mastering
    the sword, aiming to rise above karma. They have superb swordsmanship,
    are good at dual wielding, and can use magic of their own element. The
    stat needed to cast spells is intelligence.


***** <Dragoon>
--- Dragoon ---

    This class prefers swords and boasts overwhelming killing power against
    dragons. It can also use simple magic. The stat needed to cast spells is
    intelligence.


***** <Ninja>
--- Ninja ---

    A superior fighter of the dark, with high stealth and skill with swords,
    able to wield two weapons and to use magic of its own element. The stat
    needed to cast spells is intelligence.


***** <Wizard>
--- Wizard ---

    Wizards are spellcasters who draw on broad knowledge and rich experience
    to master many attack spells. In addition to Magery magic, they can use
    the magic of all four elements. The stat needed to cast spells is
    intelligence.


***** <Warlock>
--- Warlock ---

    Warlocks are scholarly spellcasters. They can use Witch magic, two
    books of Magery magic and Drakonite magic, have reasonable melee power,
    and can summon golems. The stat needed to cast spells is intelligence.


***** <Exorcist>
--- Exorcist ---

    Exorcists are specialists in slaying demons and evil spirits. By using
    Crusade magic they show unmatched strength against evil demons and the
    undead. They can also use magic of their own element. They also have
    reasonable melee power, and can find a way to victory in melee. The stat
    needed to cast spells is wisdom.


***** <Amazoness>
--- Amazoness ---

    The Amazoness is the basic class for female characters. She cannot use
    magic, but handles everything else competently. However, she is
    undeniably short on power, so it is not a good idea to raise her as an
    Amazoness to the very end.


***** <Valkyrie>
--- Valkyrie ---

    Valkyries are spellswords who excel with the spear. They are also good
    at riding. A spear thrust from horseback is devastatingly powerful. They
    can use magic of their own element. The stat needed to cast spells is
    wisdom.


***** <Archer>
--- Archer ---

    Archers are specialists with bow-type weapons. They cannot use magic,
    but their shooting ability is tremendous. They can shoot down enemies
    one after another.


***** <DragonTamer>
--- Dragon Tamer ---

    Dragon Tamers are spellswords who take up the sword themselves and fight
    alongside dragons. She is especially liked by dragons, and can borrow
    their power at any time. The stat needed to cast spells is wisdom.


***** <Witch>
--- Witch ---

    Witches are sorceresses who excel at support magic and the use of magic
    devices. They can cast Witch magic, Magery magic, and the first book of
    Death magic. The stat needed to cast spells is intelligence.


***** <Sirene>
--- Siren ---

    Sirens are beautiful sorceresses who wield many attack spells. In
    reality they are hardly different from Wizards. The stat needed to cast
    spells is intelligence.


***** <Cleric>
--- Cleric ---

    Clerics are priests who excel at healing magic. They can cast Holy magic
    and magic of their own element. Compared with other magic users, they can
    also use a bow to some extent. A Cleric can be thought of as an
    apprenticeship class before changing to Priest. The stat needed to cast
    spells is wisdom.


***** <Priest>
--- Priest ---

    Priests are clerics who wield both attack and healing magic. They can
    cast Holy magic, Crusade magic and magic of their own element. A Priest
    can be thought of as the advanced class of the Cleric. Their healing
    magic is somewhat inferior to the Cleric's. The stat needed to cast
    spells is wisdom.


***** <Lich>
--- Lich ---

    A reincarnation class taken on after death by a Wizard, Warlock,
    Exorcist, Siren or Witch who was wearing the "Ring of the Dead". Having
    been revived by the power of the Ring of the Dead, the Lich has even
    higher-level abilities. A Lich can use every magic realm except Holy,
    Symbiotic, Crusade and Drakonite, but can no longer change class. The
    stat needed to cast spells is intelligence. Liches do not need food.


***** <Angel-Knight>
--- Divine Knight ---

    A reincarnation class that a pure-hearted female character may be able
    to take on after death. Boasts high combat ability in both melee and
    magic, but can no longer change class. Divine Knights can use Crusade
    magic and magic of their own element. The stat needed to cast spells is
    wisdom. Divine Knights do not need food.


***** <High-Witch>
--- Wicce ---

    The Wicce is: "The same as a Witch? How rude. Don't lump me in with
    them. I'm prettier and curvier (snip) and the trick is to say it with a
    slight rising tone, like when you ask someone a question (heart)." This
    class can no longer change class. The stat needed to cast spells is
    intelligence.


***** <Gunner>
--- Fusilier ---

    The only class that can use the "gun", a powerful weapon made on the
    southern continent of Barbauda that takes the place of the sword. Having
    abandoned its faith in supernatural science such as magic, its
    resistance to magic has dropped remarkably, and it cannot change to any
    other class.


***** <Temple-Knight>
--- Templar ---

    Templars are knights who have sworn loyalty to the Dark Knights
    Loslorien. They can use every kind of weapon and cast Death magic. While
    in this class you cannot change to any other class. The stat needed to
    cast spells is wisdom.


***** <White-Knight>
--- White Knight ---

    White Knights are knights who have sworn loyalty to the Kingdom of New
    Xenobia. They can use every kind of weapon and cast Holy magic. While in
    this class you cannot change to any other class. The stat needed to cast
    spells is wisdom.


***** <TheElements>
=== The Elements ===

TOband has four elements: Fire, Aqua, Earth and Wind. They affect damage
calculations according to the elements of the monsters fighting the player
and the elements of the terrain. In addition, the magic realms a class can
use may be restricted by element.

The elements have opposing relationships: Fire and Aqua are opposed, and
Earth and Wind are opposed.

Many magic attributes correspond to an element. For example, fire
corresponds to Fire, cold to Aqua, acid to Earth, and so on.

All damage, from melee, magic and so on, is affected by the elements. The
way it is decided involves a rather complicated chain of several factors,
and the overall flow differs between magic attacks and everything else.

For a magic attack, if the spell has a corresponding element, the greater
the element value of that element on the terrain of both the caster and the
target, the more power the spell has. In addition, if the spell has the same
element as the caster, its power rises further, and if it has the element
opposed to the caster's, its power drops.

For an attack that is not magic, the attacker's element is used from among
the element values of the terrain of both the attacker and the one being
attacked, and the greater that element value, the more power the attack has.

To resist the damage increased by these effects, there is a defensive
modifier based on element. The greater the defender's own element value
among the element values of the terrain of the one being attacked, the
greater the rate at which damage is reduced.

If you have a means of manipulating elements, you can suitably manipulate
your own elements, the caster's, and the terrain's, so that you increase the
damage you deal while reducing the damage you take.
