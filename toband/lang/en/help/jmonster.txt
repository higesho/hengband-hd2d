=== TOband Monsters ===

There are nearly a thousand different kinds of monsters in TOband,
each of which poses a unique challenge to the adventurer. As you
progress deeper into the dungeon, the monsters will become
progressively more difficult to defeat.

***** <MonsterColors>
=== Monster Colors ===

You can often determine some information about a monster based solely
on the color it is described as having, like "a White Dragon". In
general, "White" monsters are cold-based, "Red" are fire-based, "Blue"
are electrical, "Black" are acidic, and "Green" are poisonous, and a
"Multi-Hued" monster is all of these five. "Bronze" is confusion and
"Gold" is sound. By being cold-based, we mean that the monster has
either melee, magical or breath attacks that are cold based.

Unfortunately this rule does not apply to all monsters, so don't be
surprised if that "Green" monster breathes fire at you. Note that this
rule cannot be applied to the actual color a monster is displayed as
on the screen, but only to a color in its name.


***** <MonsterMemory>
=== Monster Memories ===

It is very difficult to remember the abilities of the many different
species, but TOband automatically keeps track of your experiences with
each monster.

This feature is called the monster memory. Your monster memory recalls
the attacks of each monster (whether or not technically a monster)
which you have suffered, as well as recalling if you have observed
them to multiply or move erratically, or drop items, etc. Similarly,
if you attack a monster with a magical attack and it resists, that fact
is noted.

If you have killed enough of a particular monster, or suffered enough
attacks, recalling the monster memory may also provide you with more
detailed information, such as the monster's AC or hit points. These
are useful for judging the relative danger of each monster.

Your monster memory may be accessed by pressing '/' and then entering
the symbol you wish to research. Each monster with that symbol which
you have met will be displayed in turn, beginning with the strongest.
Alternatively, you may enter targeting mode by pressing '*' and, with
the cursor over the monster in question, press 'r' to recall details.

The best feature of your monster memory is that it can be passed on
to a new character even after you die. When you start a new
character, just use the savefile of the dead character as the base and
the monster memory will be carried over.


***** <MonsterResWeapon>
=== Monster Weapon Resistances ===

Some monsters are resistant to melee and missile attacks. There are
two kinds of resistance, to blunt weapons and to edged weapons, and two
levels of each, weak weapon resistance and strong weapon resistance.
Weak weapon resistance cuts the damage of the weapon to one half, and
strong weapon resistance cuts it to one sixth.

To describe the resistances, here is how weapons are classified as
blunt or edged.

    Blunt: hafted weapons, bullets (all firearm ammunition)
    Edged: polearms, swords, digging tools, arrows, bolts

Weapon resistance can be overcome with a blessed or an unholy weapon.
A blessed weapon ignores the weapon resistance of monsters that are
not good, and an unholy weapon ignores the weapon resistance of
monsters that are not evil. In the rare case of a weapon that is both
blessed and unholy, weapon resistance is nullified against everything.


***** <MonsterField>
=== Monster Fields ===

Some monsters fight by spreading a space called a "field" around
themselves. A field only affects those who are hostile to its owner,
and has no effect on companions who fight alongside it. Likewise, if
you spread a field yourself, your pets and friendly monsters are not
affected. The same goes in the other direction.

There are two kinds of fields: the fear field and the anti-magic field.

A fear field shakes the courage of hostile creatures and lowers the
damage modifier of their melee attacks, missile attacks, spells and
so on. A single monster's fear field may be negligible, but when
several fields overlap their effects accumulate and the damage dealt
keeps going down. This field has no effect on those who cannot feel
fear.

An anti-magic field is a very powerful field that completely prevents
hostile creatures from casting any spell and suppresses the summoning
of monsters inside the field. A creature that can fight only by magic
is rendered completely helpless. However, they can still take actions
that are not magic, such as breathing or using "techniques". Also,
when a monster that casts spells powerfully is summoned, the summoning
may succeed even inside an anti-magic field.


***** <Uniques>
=== Unique Monsters ===

TOband has normal monsters and a kind of monster called Unique (that
is, one of a kind) monsters. While normal monsters can appear many
times during the course of a game, a Unique monster will never be
generated again once you have killed it. Also, the game will never
generate two copies of the same Unique at the same time.

--- Uniques' Resistance to Magic ---

Unique monsters are more resistant to magic than other monsters. Thus,
slowing, confusing, sleeping and scaring will be less effective and may
not work at all. In addition, Genocide and Mass Genocide spells will
never work on Uniques. *Destruction* does not kill Uniques either: it
simply removes them from the current level (monsters that you have to
defeat for a quest are teleported away instead).

--- Keeping Track of Uniques ---

Since Unique monsters are often very difficult to defeat, many players
will attempt to kill them all before attempting to battle Dorgalua,
the King (itself a Unique monster). This avoids the need to battle
several very strong opponents at the same time.

Each time your character happens upon a new Unique, this is recorded
in your monster memory. Under the knowledge command ('~') is a menu
item which allows you to look at the list of Uniques you have met and
which of these have been defeated. It is a good idea to check this
periodically to gauge your progress.

--- Speaking Uniques ---

Occasionally, you will find that a Unique monster will speak to you as
it approaches. Typically, a monster will shout abuse and challenges,
but some also talk about matters closely related to themselves.

--- Rewards for Killing Uniques ---

You may also find that occasionally after killing a Unique monster,
the game will tell you that the Unique was wanted and had a bounty on
its head. If you take the monster's corpse to the Hunter's office in
Almorica Castle, you will be paid the bounty.

***** <EldritchHorror>
=== Eldritch Horrors ===

In some other Angband variants, certain monsters are so fearsome to
look upon that merely seeing them or detecting them magically makes
you lose your sanity, with serious consequences such as a temporary or
permanent drop in intelligence or wisdom, confusion, or even
mutations like fits of rage, paranoia and feeble-mindedness.

TOband does not have this sanity check. In TOband, merely seeing or
sensing a monster never costs you sanity.


***** <Pets>
=== Pets and Riding ===

Pets are very useful and also safe in TOband. It is trouble-free and
very comfortable to send your pet Great Hell Wyrm into a Troll pit and
wait outside, listening to the howls of agony and terror, the sounds
of guts splattering and bones crunching. However, you should not
expect to gain any experience or item drops for such a fight: you will
only gain experience and items for monsters to whom you deliver the
death blow yourself.

Monsters which get killed by a pet don't drop items. This feature,
unique to Hengband, the base of TOband, is a well-thought-out result.
If you try to interpret it forcibly, you might think that the pet's
rough attacks lose or destroy the items, but many people will complain
that this is not realistic. On this point, the specification was
decided by putting game balance ahead of realism.

In TOband, pets will not get angry with you even if you attack them
by mistake. You hold the free will of your pets completely under
magical control. Also, when a pet attacks other monsters with breath
or magic, it will not normally involve you and harm you. You can
concentrate on fighting enemies safely right next to your pet Great
Hell Wyrm as it spews its breath. However, if you want your pets to
give top priority to defeating the surrounding enemies even if you are
caught in their breath (for example because you are immune to the
Great Hell Wyrm's fire breath), you can tell them so with the pet
command 'p'. Just turn "allow involve player in area spell" ON.

Finally, it takes a lot of mental energy to maintain your control over
your pets. The total level of your pets is compared with an allowance
that depends on your level and your pet-upkeep skill. As long as the
total stays within the allowance, your pets are "free"; beyond that,
your mana regeneration rate drops. The larger the total of your pets'
levels, the smaller your mana regeneration rate. Unique pets are far
more expensive (about ten times their level, less for certain classes
or when you ride them). And once the upkeep exceeds 100% mana, you do
not regenerate mana but begin to lose it. If your mana is zero while
the upkeep is over 100%, you are made to dismiss pets until the upkeep
drops to 100% or less; this is also true for characters who have no
mana points. Keep this in mind if you have a pet which can summon or
otherwise produce more monsters.


--- Obtaining a pet ---

You may obtain pets in several different ways. Some magic realms offer
the ability to summon pets magically or to charm the monsters you meet.
Magical figurines can be thrown to create a pet.


--- Commanding Your Pets ---

Your pets are fairly well trained and will respond to a variety of
commands such as following you closely, staying nearby and searching
out your enemies. You can command your pets using the pet command menu,
which can be found by pressing 'p'.

The pet command menu has the following items.

dismiss pets
        Dismiss specified pets. Dismissed pets will immediately
        disappear from the current level. The game asks you whether you
        want to dismiss each pet, beginning with named pets and strong
        pets, so it is convenient to answer "N"o for the few you need
        and then dismiss all the remaining unnamed pets with "U".

specify a target of pet
        Choose a target monster for your pets to attack.

stay close
        Command your pets to stay as close to you as they can.

follow me
        Your pets follow you at a distance of a few steps.

seek and destroy
        Allow your pets to move a little way from you. Suitable for
        letting them find and attack enemies on their own.

give me space
        Your pets move away so that they do not get in your way. Useful
        when you want to fight an enemy alone for a while.

stay away
        Command your pets to go as far away from you as they can.

pets open doors
        Toggle permission of pets to open doors.

pets pick up items
        Toggle permission of pets to pick up items.

allow teleport
        Choose whether pets are allowed (or forbidden altogether) to
        teleport themselves or to teleport enemies away.

allow cast attack spell
        Choose whether pets use attack spells and breath.

allow cast disintegration spell
        Choose whether pets use attack spells and breath that destroy
        walls.

allow cast summon spell
        Choose whether pets use summoning spells.

allow involve player in area spell
        Choose whether, when pets attack monsters with breath, beams or
        ball spells, they may involve the player in the area of effect.

ride a pet
get off a pet
        You can ride horse-type and dragon-type monsters. Some classes
        are good at riding.

name pets
        Give a pet any name you like. A named pet is asked about first
        by the "dismiss pets" command, so you are less likely to
        dismiss it by mistake. There is also an option for recording
        the information of named pets in the game log.

use one hand to control a riding pet
use both hands for a weapon.
        While riding, one hand is normally used to hold the reins. If
        you carry a weapon and a shield, or fight with two weapons, you
        cannot control your mount. Therefore, even when you have only
        one weapon, you normally fight with it one-handed instead of
        two-handed. When fighting a strong monster, however, you may
        want to leave your mount to its own will and concentrate on
        attacking. With this option you can let go of the reins and
        attack with a single weapon held in both hands.


--- Riding ---

In TOband, you can ride on horse-type and dragon-type pets. To control
your mount you must keep one hand free for holding the reins, but
your pet will attack your enemies together with you, and enemy attacks
will be divided between you and your mount, so the damage you take
will be smaller.

More importantly, while you are riding, your speed does not depend on
your own original speed but on the speed of the monster you ride.
It does not matter if you quaff a potion of speed or wear a ring of
speed or boots of speed. So although you must ride a fast pet, you
gain the advantage of not having to think about speed at all when
choosing your equipment. To raise your speed temporarily, rather than
quaffing a potion of speed yourself, break it by throwing it at a wall
or dismount and throw it at your pet. There are also wands and spells
that haste a monster.

The important point here is that your riding skill level is generally
very low (it is shown by the '_' command). A player with a low skill
level cannot make full use of the speed of the monster being ridden,
and is also more likely to fall off.


***** <Friendly>
=== Friendly Monsters ===

You will occasionally find a monster in the dungeon that isn't out to
get you. Such monsters are described in game messages as being
"friendly". A friendly monster will not attempt to hurt you but neither
will it necessarily attack your enemies.

If you do something nasty to a friendly monster it will turn against
you. Any monsters that a friendly monster summons will also be
friendly. Unlike pets, however, friendly monsters require no mana
upkeep and cannot be commanded.
