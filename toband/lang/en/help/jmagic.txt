=== The TOband Magic System ===

The magic available to players in TOband is quite different from the
magic systems of ZAngband and Hengband. All spells are cast with the
'm' command.

You will often be unable to tell what a spell does just by looking at
its name or casting it a few times. In that case, use the command that
shows spell descriptions ('b'/'P') and select the spell you want to
know about. A few lines of description will be displayed.

--- Spellbook magic ---

There are 11 realms of spellbooks: Magery, Fire, Aqua, Earth, Wind,
Holy, Death, Symbiotic, Witch, Drakonite and Crusade. As you gain
levels, you become able to read new, more powerful spells written in
the spellbooks you can use. You must always carry the spellbook which
contains the spell you want to cast. Except for the Drakonite books,
all spellbooks can only be obtained in stores.

The realms you can use are determined by your class. Depending on the
class, however, the realm may be restricted by the player's element.
Also, for realms with several spellbooks, some classes cannot read the
higher-level books. In the table below, 'O' means that the class can
use the realm, and 'E' means that the class can use the realm only if
it matches your own element.

***** <MagicRealms>
=== The Realms of Spellbook Magic ===

***** <Magery>
--- Magery ---
     Magery is the basic realm of the mage-type classes, excelling at
     detection, identification and attack.

***** <Fire>
--- Fire ---
     Fire magic. It has a full set of fire-based attack spells. Also,
     many of its spells are affected by the Fire element.

***** <Aqua>
--- Aqua ---
     Water magic. It has a full set of water-based attack spells.
     Also, many of its spells are affected by the Aqua element.

***** <Earth>
--- Earth ---
     Earth magic. It has a full set of earth-based attack spells.
     Also, many of its spells are affected by the Earth element.

***** <Wind>
--- Wind ---
     Wind magic. It has a full set of wind-based attack spells. Also,
     many of its spells are affected by the Wind element.

***** <Holy>
--- Holy ---
     Holy magic excels at recovery. It contains many healing,
     defensive and detection spells, though it has a few attack spells
     as well. It is said that some of its high-level spells have the
     power to reduce undead to dust.

***** <Death>
--- Death ---
     There is no more evil category than the black magic of Death.
     These spells are relatively hard to learn, but at higher levels
     they give the caster the power to control the living and the
     undead at will. Unfortunately, the most powerful spells need the
     caster's own blood as their catalyst and often hurt the caster
     during the casting.

***** <Symbiotic>
--- Symbiotic ---
     Symbiotic is a realm of magic that puts weight on helping each
     other with your pets. It can heal your pets and summon pets.

***** <Witch>
--- Witch ---
     Witch is a realm of support magic. It has hardly any attack
     spells, but it has many spells that strengthen your own
     abilities or affect the elements. At higher levels it also has
     spells that can temporarily change the element of yourself or of
     an enemy.

***** <Drakonite>
--- Drakonite ---
     Drakonite magic is a realm developed by an ancient civilization
     and now lost. Its attack spells have terrifying destructive
     power, and its other spells produce unbelievable effects.

***** <Crusade>
--- Crusade ---
     Crusade is the magic of "justice". It contains many spells that
     directly hurt enemies, with fearsome power against evil enemies
     in particular. It is not very effective against good enemies,
     however.


***** <CastingSpells>
=== On Casting Spells ===

Players who select spellcasting classes may notice a few unusual
phenomena during the course of the game. Here are a few hints on what
may be happening.

--- Armor and Spell Casting ---

All spellcasting classes are penalized for wearing armor above a
certain total weight. Typically, the more reliant on spells your class
is, or the more it prefers to fight in light armor, the lower your
weight allowance. If you exceed your weight limit, your mana will
drop. This penalty is fairly stiff for low level characters but can
generally be ignored by high level ones.

--- Spellcasting Classes and Gloves ---

Some of the classes that cast spells from spellbooks are penalized for
wearing armor on their hands. The exceptions are armor that raises
dexterity and armor that grants free action or makes magical devices
easier to use; wearing those results in no penalty. The rationale
behind this is that the hands must make complicated gestures when
casting a spell, and anything encumbering the hands would interfere
with that. The magic of other classes is cast by praying, so those
classes are not penalized.

--- Casting Spells With Insufficient Mana ---

Occasionally, you may try (either by accident or in desperation) to
cast a spell when you have insufficient mana to do so. In these
circumstances you will be told by the game that you do not have enough
mana to cast the spell. If the over_exert option ("Allow casting
spells when short of mana") is on, you will then be asked "Attempt it
anyway?"; if it is off, you simply cannot cast the spell. Casting the
spell under these conditions has a drastically reduced chance of
success. Whenever the casting is actually carried out with too little
mana, you faint from the effort: you are paralyzed for several turns
(even if you have free action), and there is a 50% chance that your
constitution is also damaged (in a quarter of those cases the damage is
permanent). The paralysis and the constitution damage happen
regardless of whether the casting was successful or not.

--- Spell Durations ---

Some spells, such as speed or resist-all spells, grant an effect which
wears off after a certain period of time. With very few exceptions,
multiple castings of such spells are not cumulative in terms of the
duration of the spell. In other words, casting a spell which has a
duration of 20 turns three times will not result in a duration of 60
turns. Typically, the duration of the last casting is the one that
takes effect.


***** <SpellTypes>
=== Spell Types ===

--- Bolts and Beams ---

A bolt spell is aimed in a direction or at a target, and will hit the
first monster or obstruction in that direction. This happens whether
you intend it or not. Occasionally, you may notice that your bolt spell
hits multiple monsters along the line of fire. This is because some
bolt spells are granted a chance of "beaming", which may be either
fixed or level-dependent. A beam will hit every enemy within range
along a straight line in the direction in which it was aimed.

--- Balls ---

There are two things to remember about ball spells. Firstly, unlike
bolt spells, they can be aimed at a specific target. You can target a
specific monster rather than the first monster in the direction you
are aiming; that is, you can fire at an enemy in the middle of a pack
of monsters. Secondly, they have a radius (which varies from spell to
spell). They affect not only the target but also the enemies (and
objects, etc.) within that radius around the target. In general,
however, the strength of the effect weakens the farther it is from the
center. Ghostly monsters are sometimes inside walls; a ball spell
aimed at such a monster will blow up in front of the wall and do only
half the damage of a direct hit. Note that some balls do not weaken
with distance.

--- Line-of-Sight ---

Line-of-sight spells affect all monsters that are currently in sight of
your character. This includes monsters that may not currently be
visible because of darkness but would be if it were lit.

--- Area ---

Area spells affect an area around the player. The size of the area can
vary considerably from spell to spell. For example, Light Area lights
up a whole room, Detect Traps and Mass Genocide affect the inside of a
circle of a specific radius, and normal Genocide affects the entire
level.
