=== Gambling Rules ===

Between: Three 10-sided dice are rolled; 2 black, 1 red. The red die must
       fall between the two black dice to win. If the red die matches a
       black die, you lose. Pays 4x.
Craps: Two dice are rolled. On the first roll, a 7 or 11 wins. A 2, 3 or 12
       loses. Otherwise, keep rolling until the first roll is matched (win)
       or a 7 is rolled (loss). Pays 2x.
Wheel: Pick a number from 0-9. If that number comes up, you win. Pays 9x.
Slots: Three dice are rolled and shown as symbols. A match wins. The symbols
       are Lemon, Orange, Sword, Shield, Plum and Cherry (Lemon comes up most
       often, Cherry least often). Payoffs are as follows:
       Lemon Lemon               2
       Lemon Lemon Lemon         5
       Orange Orange Orange     10
       Sword Sword Sword        20
       Shield Shield Shield     50
       Plum Plum Plum          200
       Cherry Cherry Cherry   1000
