=== Overview of TOband's Towns ===

A town is made up of stores that deal in items and buildings that offer
other services.

General Store (1): Sells food, torches, arrows, bolts and other necessities.
Armoury (2): Gear to protect you from the dangers of the dungeon is sold here.
Weaponsmith's Shop (3): Deals in bladed weapons and polearms.
Temple (4): Sells holy items such as potions that heal wounds.
Alchemy Shop (5): Sells various kinds of scrolls and potions.
Magic Shop (6): Sells wands, staffs, rings, amulets and the like.
Black Market (7): Prices are high, but there are more useful goods.
Home (8): Keep your precious belongings here. It can be entered from any town.
Bookstore (9): Deals in various spellbooks.
Museum (0): Donate things you do not need but wish to keep as a memento.

Inn: Offers lodging and meals. Also offers teleportation between towns.
Church and Castle: You can accept quests here.
Temple (+): Restores drained stats.
Fighters Hall: Enhances weapons and armor.
Paladins Guild: Enhances armor here and heals you.
Archers Guild: Enhances arrows, shots and bows.
Thieves Guild: Identifies your belongings.
Beastmaster: Tells you about monsters' abilities and resistances.
Tower of Trump Magic: For a large sum, returns you to any floor of the dungeon.
Tower of Sorcery: Recharges wands and staffs.
Library: Here, items are *identified* for you.
Weaponmaster: Examines the damage your weapon deals.
Casino: Read the rules before you play. The games are not rigged.
Arena: Fight the opponents set against you; keep winning to become champion.
Hunter's office: Bring a bounty target's corpse here to exchange it for gold.
Chaos Tower: Cures mutations. Gives quests.
Tower of Hermit: Recharges wands and staffs. Gives quests.
Nature Tower: Performs a ritual to return your element to neutral. Gives
    quests.
